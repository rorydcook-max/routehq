The deposit secures the Renter's obligations: unpaid rent, damage up to the amount the Renter is liable for, fuel, mileage, cleaning, late return, lost keys or accessories, traffic fines and any other amount owed under this agreement.

The Owner returns the deposit, less any amounts properly deducted, after the vehicle has been returned and checked. The Owner gives the Renter a written list of any deduction with photos, receipts or quotes. If the amounts owed are more than the deposit, the Renter pays the difference within 7 days of being told.

Traffic fines often arrive after a rental ends. The Owner may keep a reasonable part of the deposit for up to 30 days to cover fines the Owner has reason to expect, and must return it promptly if none arrive.

The Owner will not keep the Renter's original passport or national ID card as a deposit. The Owner may take a copy or photo of them to identify the Renter (section 17).

## 7. Cancellation and early return

Cancellation by the Renter before the start time: the Owner keeps any booking payment that this agreement or the booking confirmation says is non-refundable, and refunds the rest.

Early return: rent for the booked period is not refunded if the Renter returns the vehicle early.

If the Owner cannot provide the vehicle (or a similar or better one) at the start time, the Renter may cancel and receive a full refund of everything paid.

## 8. Fuel, charging and mileage

Fuel policy: same-to-same. The vehicle must be returned with at least the fuel (or battery) level it had at handover.

Missing fuel is charged at the local pump price plus a refuelling service fee of ____________ ____________. Using the wrong fuel is the Renter's responsibility, including towing and repair.

Mileage: unlimited, within the permitted area (section 10).

The Renter must not tamper with the odometer, speed limiter or GPS tracker. Tampering is a serious breach (section 15).

## 9. Safe and lawful use

The Renter must drive or ride carefully, obey all traffic laws, and keep the vehicle locked with the keys removed whenever it is left. Keys must never be left in or on the vehicle.

Motorbikes and scooters: the rider and any passenger must wear a fastened helmet at all times. No more than one passenger, and no passenger who cannot reach the footrests safely.

Cars: every occupant must wear a seatbelt, children must use a suitable child seat where the law requires one, and the number of passengers must not exceed the number of seats.

It is forbidden to: drive under the influence of alcohol or drugs, or above the legal limit; use a phone while driving except hands-free; race, perform stunts or drive off-road (unless the vehicle is meant for it and the Owner agreed); tow or push anything; carry goods for payment, illegal goods or dangerous loads; use the vehicle for ride-hailing, delivery work, driving lessons or to rent it to anyone else; let anyone not named in section 2 drive or ride.

## 10. Permitted area

|---|---|

The vehicle must stay within the permitted area above. Taking it further, on a ferry or to another island, needs the Owner's written permission first, and the Renter pays any ferry fees, delays and recovery costs.

The vehicle must not leave the country under any circumstances. Insurance does not cover it outside the country or outside the permitted area.

The vehicle may be fitted with a GPS tracker, used only for security, recovery and checking that this agreement is kept.

## 11. Insurance

| Insurance on this vehicle | |
|---|---|
| Renter's excess (most the Renter pays per incident, if the rules are kept) | |

The vehicle has the compulsory insurance required by law, which covers injury to people and, depending on the country, limited damage to others. It does not cover damage to the rented vehicle itself.

The vehicle has only the compulsory insurance. The Renter is responsible for damage to the rented vehicle, its theft, and damage to other people's property (section 12).

Insurance does not protect the Renter, and the Renter pays in full, when damage or loss happens: while driving under the influence, without a valid licence for the vehicle, by a driver not named in section 2, outside the permitted area, during forbidden use (section 9), through deliberate or reckless acts, or when keys were left with the vehicle. Tyres, wheels, glass, underbody, interior, keys and accessories are often excluded by insurers and are the Renter's responsibility unless the Owner confirms in writing that they are covered.

Personal belongings left in or on the vehicle are not insured by the Owner. The Owner is not responsible for their loss or damage.

## 12. Damage, theft and loss

The Renter is responsible for the vehicle from handover until it is returned and checked. The condition at handover is recorded in Annex A with photos; anything not recorded there is treated as new unless it is normal wear and tear.

Repair costs are based on a quote or invoice from a garage, or on the damage price list in Annex B where one is attached. The Renter may ask to see the quote, and may get a second quote from a garage the Owner approves within 3 days.

If the vehicle or its keys are stolen, the Renter must report it to the police at once and give the Owner the police report. Where the theft happened because the vehicle was left unlocked, with the keys, or outside the permitted area, the Renter pays the value of the vehicle as stated in Annex B (or its market value).

Lost keys, remote controls, helmets, documents, number plates and accessories are charged at their replacement cost, plus any locksmith or re-registration cost.

## 13. Accidents and breakdowns

In an accident, the Renter must: make the area safe and get medical help if anyone is hurt; call the Owner straight away on the number in section 1; call the police and the insurer's hotline when the Owner or the law requires it; take photos of all vehicles, plates, the place and any damage; note the other party's name, phone, plate and insurance; and not admit fault, pay or accept money without the Owner's agreement.

Breakdowns: the Renter must stop safely, tell the Owner and follow the Owner's instructions. The Renter must not have the vehicle repaired without the Owner's permission, except for minor emergency costs up to ____________ ____________, which the Owner refunds against a receipt.

If the vehicle stops working through no fault of the Renter, the Owner will repair it or provide a similar vehicle within 24 hours of being told where reasonably possible. If the Owner cannot, the Renter may end the rental and receive a refund of the rent for the unused days.

The Renter must report any warning light, unusual noise, puncture or fault promptly and must not keep using a vehicle that is unsafe.

## 14. Fines, tolls and parking

The Renter pays all traffic and parking fines, tolls, towing, impound and clamping costs arising during the rental, including fines that reach the Owner after the vehicle is returned, plus a handling fee of ____________ ____________ per fine.

The Owner may give the Renter's name and details to the police or the issuing authority so that a fine is transferred to the Renter, where the law allows.

## 15. Ending the agreement by the Owner

The Owner may end this agreement and take the vehicle back at once if the Renter: does not pay on time; gave false details; lets an unnamed person drive; uses the vehicle unlawfully or against section 9; takes it outside the permitted area without permission; tampers with the odometer or tracker; abandons it; or puts the vehicle or other people at serious risk. The Owner may then deduct what the Renter owes under this agreement, including reasonable recovery costs, and refunds any remaining prepaid rent for the days after the vehicle is recovered.

The Renter must make the vehicle available for collection, and pays reasonable recovery costs where the Owner has to fetch it.

## 16. Returning the vehicle

The vehicle must be returned with its keys, documents and accessories, with the fuel level agreed in section 8, and reasonably clean. Both parties check the vehicle together and record the condition, odometer and fuel in Annex A with photos. If the Renter is not present at the check, the Owner's dated photos are accepted as the record.

A vehicle returned with excessive dirt, sand, mud, stains, food or odour inside may be charged the reasonable cost of cleaning.

## 17. Personal data and documents

The Owner collects the Renter's and drivers' names, contact details, ID and licence details, copies of documents and photos of the vehicle only to provide this rental, prove identity, comply with the law, handle fines, claims and disputes, and keep business records. The Owner keeps them only as long as needed for those purposes and the law, keeps them secure, and does not sell them.

The Renter agrees that copies of their documents and the inspection photos and videos may be used as evidence of identity and of the vehicle's condition, and may be given to insurers, the police and authorities when needed for a claim, fine or legal requirement.

## 18. General terms

Messages: notices under this agreement may be sent by phone, message app (WhatsApp, LINE, Telegram, Zalo or similar), SMS or email to the contact details in sections 1 and 2, and count as received when sent.

Force majeure: neither party is responsible for a delay or failure caused by events outside their control, such as natural disasters, floods, storms, government orders or ferry cancellations. Rent for days the vehicle could not be used because of such an event is agreed fairly between the parties.

Whole agreement: this agreement, its annexes and any special conditions are the whole agreement. Changes are valid only in writing (a message from both parties is enough). If any part is found invalid, the rest still applies.

Language: this agreement is written in English. If it is translated, the English version applies.

Governing law: this agreement is governed by the laws of the country where the vehicle is rented. The parties will first try to settle any dispute by discussion; if they cannot, the competent courts of the place where the vehicle was rented decide.
