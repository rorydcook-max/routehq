Navotar was a well-known name in car rental software, but you won't find it under that name any more. [Auto Rental News reported](https://www.autorentalnews.com/news/navotar-announces-branding-switch-to-rentall) in May 2022 that Navotar had switched its operations and products to Rentall, and navotar.com now redirects to [rentallsoftware.com](https://www.rentallsoftware.com/). So this page compares RENTALL with RoveHQ.

The short version: RENTALL is a quote-based system for fleets of 10 vehicles or more, across many vehicle types. RoveHQ is a self-serve, phone-first tool for small fleets of cars, scooters, motorbikes, quads and vans, including those with fewer than 10 vehicles.

Facts about RENTALL come from its own website **as of November 2026**.

## RENTALL in brief

RENTALL's [homepage](https://www.rentallsoftware.com/) says "thousands of customers across 70+ countries" use it. Its menus list solutions for cars, RVs, car sharing, construction machinery, scooters, coaches and buses, planes, buggies, boats, electric vehicles and dealership loaners.

The [pricing page](https://www.rentallsoftware.com/pricing/) doesn't show prices. You fill in your vehicle type, fleet size, region and number of locations, and RENTALL emails a quote. There are three packages: **Essential** (the rental software, two training sessions and PDF configuration, the mobile app and card processing), **Business** (adds the online reservation plugin) and **Enterprise**. The page also says: "Our minimum fleet size required to implement RENTALL is 10 vehicles", with exceptions for expected growth and other factors.

## Where RENTALL is stronger

- **Range of vehicle types.** From scooters and cars to coaches, boats, planes and heavy machinery, in one system.
- **Larger operations.** Multi-location support, add-on modules, custom development and an API to build your own branded app.
- **Its own payment processing.** Card processing through RENTALL Payments comes in every package, with advance payments at booking through the reservation plugin.
- **Training included.** Two training sessions and PDF configuration come with every package.
- **A native mobile app** for check-out and check-in.

## Where RoveHQ is stronger

- **Fleets under 10.** No minimum fleet size, and a free plan for up to 2 cars and 5 motorbikes.
- **Published prices and self-serve.** You can see the price, including the platform fee on card payments, and start today without a quote or sales call.
- **Online booking on every plan.** A booking page and website booking box where customers pay by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or European methods, with deposits held on the card.
- **Agreements and ID on a phone.** An agreement for each booking in English and the local language in 18 countries, passport and license photos read for you, and handover photos with a damage diagram and signature.
- **Every way customers pay.** Card and wallets online, and cash, transfer or QR with slips checked against what's owed (including PromptPay and QRIS), with LINE and Telegram in one inbox.

## Who should pick which

**Choose RENTALL if** you have 10 or more vehicles, perhaps of several types or across several locations, you want custom development or your own branded app, and you're happy to go through a quote and training.

**Choose RoveHQ if** you have a small fleet of cars, scooters, motorbikes, quads or vans, especially fewer than 10, you want to start without a sales process, and you want card payments, deposits, agreements and handovers handled from your phone.

## Switching from Navotar or RENTALL

We couldn't find a public help article on exports, so our [guide to switching from Navotar](/switch/navotar) gives general steps: look for Excel export on your reservation and agreement lists or reports, or ask support for a copy of your data. RoveHQ recognizes reservation exports with agreement numbers, vehicle types and pickup and return dates, converts miles to your unit, and asks you to confirm date order. Importing is free, and you can undo it within 30 days.
