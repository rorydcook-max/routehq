HQ Rental Software is one of the best-known car rental systems for independent operators. Its [homepage](https://hqrentalsoftware.com/) says it's trusted by 1,250+ operators in 85+ countries. RoveHQ is a newer, phone-first tool for small fleets of cars, scooters, motorbikes, quads and vans. Both publish their prices, which makes a fair comparison easier.

All facts about HQ come from its own website **as of November 2026**. Prices and features change, so check their pages before deciding.

## HQ Rental Software in brief

HQ describes itself as built for car and van rental, adapting to motorbikes, boats, equipment and long-term leasing "with a few simple configurations". Its [FAQ](https://hqrentalsoftware.com/faqs) says it serves "a small operator with 5 cars moving from Turo to direct bookings" through to "a large enterprise running 2,000 vehicles across 10 locations".

The [pricing page](https://hqrentalsoftware.com/pricing) lists:

| Plan | Monthly | Vehicles | One-time fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | $120 | 10 | $550 |
| Standard | $175 | 25 | $950 |
| Professional | $250 | 50 | $1,500 |
| Enterprise | Price calculator | 50+ | $1,500 |

Each plan includes one branch, with extra branches at $50 a month, and annual billing saves up to 8%. The FAQ says the software is available in more than 30 languages, hosting is in the USA, the Netherlands or Singapore, and there's a mobile app for iOS and Android. Most small operators go live "within a few days", and HQ's team helps import data from spreadsheets or other systems.

## Where HQ is stronger

- **Booking channels.** Channel management with sites such as Expedia, Kayak and Turo. If you sell to travelers through booking sites, this matters.
- **Choice of payment gateway.** Stripe, PayPal, Braintree, Mollie and more, where RoveHQ works with Stripe only.
- **Integrations.** QuickBooks and Xero, telematics from Geotab, Invers and Zubie, and US toll systems.
- **Growth.** Multi-branch control, KPI dashboards and a path to hundreds of vehicles on one system.
- **A native app** for pickups and returns, plus hosting close to your customers.

## Where RoveHQ is stronger

- **Cost of getting started.** No setup fee, a free plan with no time limit, and paid plans from US$15 a month. You sign up and set it up yourself in minutes, with no sales call.
- **Small fleets and two-wheelers.** Cars and motorbikes are counted separately, so a shop with 8 motorbikes fits Starter at $15 a month, where HQ's smallest plan is $120 a month plus $550 up front.
- **Agreements.** A rental agreement in English and the local language in 18 countries across Southeast Asia and Europe (Greek, Spanish, Italian, Thai, Indonesian and more), written for you and signed on the customer's phone.
- **Phone-first.** Every task, including handover photos, damage marking, signatures and card deposit holds, is designed for a phone screen.
- **Every way customers pay.** Card and wallet payments online, plus cash, transfer and QR codes with slips checked against what's owed (including PromptPay and QRIS), and LINE and Telegram in one inbox with automatic reminders.

## What the first year might cost

Using HQ's published prices and RoveHQ's plans, and leaving out each payment provider's own processing fees, which apply on both:

- **12 cars** need HQ Standard: $175 × 12 + $950 = **$3,050 in year one**. On RoveHQ, 12 cars fit Pro at $30 a month, or $330 a year on yearly billing (11 months). Add RoveHQ's platform fee on payments through Stripe: if you take $4,000 a month by card, that's 1% on a yearly plan ($480 a year, so **about $810 in total**) or 2% on Pro paid monthly ($960 a year, so **about $1,320 in total**).
- **8 motorbikes** fit HQ Basic: $120 × 12 + $550 = **$1,990 in year one**. On RoveHQ they fit Starter at $15 a month ($165 a year on yearly billing), plus 1% of card payments on yearly billing or 2.5% paid monthly.

Those numbers aren't the whole story. HQ includes channel management, a wider choice of gateways and integrations that RoveHQ doesn't have, and the more you take by card, the more RoveHQ's platform fee counts. ## Who should pick which

**Choose HQ Rental Software if** you run a growing car or van business with 10 to 50 vehicles or more, you want bookings from Expedia or Turo, and integrations with QuickBooks, Xero or telematics matter. The 30+ languages and Singapore hosting help in Asia.

**Choose RoveHQ if** you have a small fleet of cars, scooters, motorbikes, quads or vans and want to run it from your phone, with card payments and deposits online, agreements written for you and no setup fee. You can start on the free plan today.
