Go to **Reports** and tap **Fleet value**.

At the top you see what the whole fleet is **Worth now**, how many vehicles have a value, and, for those where you've entered a purchase price, whether they're worth more or less than you paid in total.

## Get estimates

Tap **Estimate values**. RoveHQ looks at used-vehicle adverts for the same make, model and year and gives a figure for each one. It takes about half a minute per vehicle; vehicles of the same model and year share one look-up. To check a single vehicle, tap **Estimate** (or **Check again**) on its card.

Values checked in the last month aren't checked again. If some can't be valued, try later or enter a value yourself.

## Each vehicle shows

- its value, or **Not valued yet**, and **Your figure** if you entered it yourself
- the advert range it came from, like "Adverts: ฿380,000 to ฿420,000 (6 found, ...)". With fewer than 3 adverts it says **Rough estimate**.
- its condition. If none is set, estimates assume good condition. Damage found at a return moves it down by itself.
- what you paid and how much it's gone up or down
- the sum insured, flagged when it's well below what the vehicle is worth

## Make the figures better

Tap **Add what you paid** or **Edit figures** to enter the purchase price, date bought, value, condition and sum insured. Adding the year gives a closer estimate.
