Rentle is now called TWICE Commerce. TWICE's documentation says every table in its admin exports the current view with one click, keeping your filters, sorts and visible columns, so show every column you want before you export.

RoveHQ recognizes TWICE order exports (customers, line items, price, stage, sales channel) and older Rentle order exports with a store, start and end times, customer and total, and reads each order as a booking. It also recognizes the Payments report by its columns (payment date, type, method, total), and reads each payment as money in or out.
