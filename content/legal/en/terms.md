## 6. Shared customer history

RoveHQ lets businesses warn each other about serious problems with renters.

- You may report a renter only for a rental that **really happened and was handed over**, and only for one of these: unpaid rent, damage, a vehicle not returned, or false documents.
- Everything you report must be **true and accurate**. Don't report disputes you haven't tried to settle, and update or withdraw a report when it's settled.
- Other businesses on RoveHQ that check the same person will see the report and **your business name** as the one that made it.
- Renters can reply to or dispute a report. **RoveHQ may remove a report** at any time, for example if it looks wrong, is disputed without evidence, or is old.
- You use reports as one piece of information, not as the only reason to refuse someone. You're responsible for your own decisions and for having a lawful basis (usually your legitimate interest in preventing fraud and loss) for making and checking reports.

## 7. Shared rental prices

RoveHQ combines the prices businesses set for each vehicle model into **unnamed market figures** (for example, a typical daily price for a model in an area). A figure is shown only when **three or more businesses** have that model, and it never names a business or a renter. By using RoveHQ you agree that your prices may be included this way.

- If you turn on a public booking page or a directory listing, your **business name, vehicles, photos, prices and availability are shown publicly** on RoveHQ's sites (including rove.rent) and may be found by search engines.
- Keep them accurate. Honor bookings made at the prices and availability you showed.
- When a renter books through RoveHQ's marketplace, **a service fee applies**. The fee and how it's charged are shown in the app before you turn the marketplace on, and on each booking. Bookings through your own booking link carry no RoveHQ fee unless your plan says otherwise.

## 9. The renter list

Booking forms include an **optional** box, "Send me trip extras". It is RoveHQ's own request to the renter, not yours: a renter who ticks it agrees that RoveHQ keeps its own copy of their details and rental history to send them partner offers. RoveHQ is the controller of that copy, under our [privacy policy](/legal/privacy). The box is never ticked in advance, it doesn't affect the booking, and the renter can untick it at any time, which deletes RoveHQ's copy. Please don't tick it for a renter or tell them they must.
