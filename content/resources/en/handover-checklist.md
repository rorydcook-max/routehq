Most deposit arguments come down to one question: was that scratch there before? This checklist gives you a written answer, signed by the renter, before they drive away and again when they bring the vehicle back.

It is built for small rental businesses: a few cars, a row of scooters, or a mix of both. Print a stack, keep them on a clipboard at the counter, and fill one in for every rental.

## Who it's for

- Car and scooter rental shops that still do handovers on paper
- Operators who rent by the day, week or month and want the same routine every time
- Anyone who has lost a deposit dispute because nobody wrote down the fuel level

## How to use it

1. **Fill in the header before the renter arrives.** Business name, booking dates, plate number and deposit amount. It saves five minutes at the counter.
2. **Walk round the vehicle together.** Use the car or scooter grid and mark each mark you find with a letter: S for scratch, D for dent, C for chip or crack, M for missing. Ask the renter to look as well.
3. **Take the 12 photos on the list.** Include the odometer and fuel gauge in one shot each, and make sure your phone saves the date and time.
4. **Both of you initial each stage and sign.** The renter keeps a photo of the signed sheet; you keep the original with the agreement.
5. **Repeat at return using the right-hand column.** Anything new goes in the notes box with the photo numbers, before the deposit is refunded.

Keep the sheet with your [free rental agreement](/resources/rental-agreement), which should say that the handover checklist forms part of the contract. If the agreement and the checklist agree, a dispute is usually short.
