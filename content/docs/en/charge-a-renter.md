You paid for a repair, a fine or fuel, and the customer should cover it. You record it once: it goes in as your cost, and the customer gets the bill.

## From the booking

1. Open the booking and tap **More** > **Add a cost**.
2. Choose the type (repair, fine, fuel and so on), enter what you paid, the date, and add the receipt if you have one.
3. **Charge this to the customer** is already ticked, with this booking picked.
4. Enter the **Amount to bill**. It can be different from what you paid.
5. Save.

## What happens to the money

- If you still hold a deposit, the charge comes out of it first. RoveHQ shows how much, e.g. "€200 comes out of the deposit held (€500)".
- If the charge is more than the deposit, tick **Bill the customer the other ...** to put the rest on their booking. Untick it if insurance or someone else is paying.
- The customer is told, and can pay the bill from their booking page. When they pay, it's money in.
- Your cost stays as money out either way.

## Traffic fines

Fines often arrive weeks later. Open the vehicle and tap **Record a fine**. Set the date of the offense: whoever had the vehicle that day is billed, and it comes out of their deposit if you still hold it.

## From any cost

On **Record money in or out**, when you add a repair, servicing, fuel, fine, accessories or other cost on a vehicle that has bookings, you'll see **Charge this to the customer**. Tick it and pick the booking.

## At the return check

Missing fuel and new damage can also be charged straight from the return check. See [Handover and return checks](/docs/handover-checks).

## Common questions

**What if the customer never pays?**
On the booking's **Payments** tab, tap **Won't be paid** next to the charge. The cost stays as money out.
