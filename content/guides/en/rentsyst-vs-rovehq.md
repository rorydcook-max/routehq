RentSyst and RoveHQ are both built for vehicle rental, which makes this a closer comparison than most. RentSyst is a cloud system that also covers car sharing and taxi fleets, with GPS and telematics at its core. RoveHQ is a phone-first tool for small fleets, from one vehicle to a few dozen cars, scooters, motorbikes, quads or vans.

Everything about RentSyst here comes from its own website **as of November 2026**. Check their site for the latest.

## RentSyst in brief

RentSyst's [homepage](https://rentsyst.com/) calls it a "cloud CRM system for all types of car rental business". It highlights GPS tracking with remote control of door locks and the engine, a timeline and daily plan for reservations, and financial tracking across managers. It's a web application, so you can use it from a phone or computer.

The [pricing page](https://rentsyst.com/price/) is unusually clear. Beginner is a flat €55 a month for up to 15 vehicles. After that you pay per vehicle: €3 (16 to 50), €2.70 (51 to 100), €2.50 (101 to 200) and €2 (201 to 400), with Enterprise by arrangement. Optional one-off packages include training (€50), onboarding (€150), setup (€100) and integration (€300). There's a 14-day free version.

For payments, RentSyst's FAQ has integration guides for [Stripe](https://rentsyst.com/th/faq/question/137/), [Paysera](https://rentsyst.com/faq/question/163/) and others. Its own [comparison with Booqable](https://blog.rentsyst.com/en/post/rentsyst-vs-booqable/) says it handles motorcycles, boats, RVs and bikes as well as cars.

## Where RentSyst is stronger

- **GPS and telematics.** Built into the same system as your bookings, with movement history and remote controls on supported hardware.
- **Choice of payment provider.** Stripe plus several European gateways such as Paysera and ePay. RoveHQ works with Stripe only.
- **Scale.** Published pricing up to 400 vehicles, unlimited managers on higher plans, dynamic pricing, loyalty and API access.
- **Value at 10 to 15 vehicles.** €55 a month flat for up to 15 vehicles is hard to beat if you need GPS too.

## Where RoveHQ is stronger

- **Very small fleets.** Free for up to 2 cars and 5 motorbikes, with no time limit, and €13 (US$15, £11) a month for up to 5 cars and 10 motorbikes. Cars and motorbikes are counted separately, so a scooter-heavy shop pays less.
- **Set up yourself.** Sign up and start in a few minutes, with no onboarding package to buy, and import your RentSyst data free.
- **Handover on a phone.** Odometer read from a photo, fuel, walkaround video, damage diagram and signature, compared automatically at return. Hold a deposit on the customer's card and release or capture it at return.
- **Agreements and ID.** An agreement for every booking in English and the local language in 18 countries across Southeast Asia and Europe, signed on the customer's phone, with passport and license photos read for you.
- **Every way customers pay.** Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay and European methods online, and cash, transfer or QR with slips checked against what's owed (including PromptPay and QRIS).
- **Languages and chat.** 28 languages for staff and customers; LINE and Telegram in one inbox with automatic reminders.

## Who should pick which

**Choose RentSyst if** GPS tracking and telematics matter to you, you want a European payment gateway other than Stripe, or you have 15 vehicles or more and expect to grow. It also suits car sharing and taxi fleets.

**Choose RoveHQ if** you run a small shop with up to a few dozen cars, scooters, motorbikes, quads or vans and want to work from your phone: card payments and deposits online, agreements written for you, license checks and handover photos, with a free plan to start.
