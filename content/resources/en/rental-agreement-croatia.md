Croatia's coast road to Dubrovnik [crosses Bosnia and Herzegovina](https://www.rac.co.uk/drive/travel/country/croatia) at Neum, which many renters don't realise is leaving the country. This agreement makes it a permitted crossing only with your written permission and a Green Card.

## What the Croatia version covers

**Alcohol.** Zero tolerance for drivers under 25, new drivers and professionals: a real point for young renters.

**Equipment.** Dipped headlights in daylight during winter time, and a reflective vest, warning triangle and first-aid kit in the car.

**Tolls and fines.** Motorway tolls (no vignette), and fines issued to the renter when the police ask you for their details.

**Language.** Croatian law requires consumer information in Croatian and Latin script ([Consumer Protection Act](https://www.zakon.hr/z/193/zakon-o-zastiti-potrosaca)), so Croatian sits beside the English.

Every EU version also follows EU consumer law: prices shown with every compulsory charge included, damage charged only at its evidenced cost and never above the excess when a damage waiver applies, no keeping the whole deposit as a penalty, and the [14-day withdrawal right not applying to car rental for set dates](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2011/83/oj). Fines and renter details follow the GDPR, and the renter signs on paper or electronically, both valid under [eIDAS](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2014/910/oj).
