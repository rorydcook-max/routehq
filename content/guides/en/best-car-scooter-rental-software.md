There's no single best rental software. There's the one that fits how many vehicles you have, what you rent, how your customers pay and how you like to work. This roundup covers the tools small car, scooter, quad and van rental shops most often compare, with prices and facts from each company's own website **as of November 2026**.

> We make RoveHQ, and it's in this list. We've tried to describe every product fairly, from its own website, and to be clear when another tool is the better choice. Prices change, so check each site before you decide.

## The short list

- **RoveHQ**: best for small fleets of cars, scooters, motorbikes, quads or vans, run from a phone. Free plan, card payments and deposits online, agreements written for you.
- **Booqable**: best for equipment rental with some bikes or scooters, and a polished online shop.
- **RentSyst**: best value for 10 to 15 vehicles if you want GPS and telematics.
- **HQ Rental Software**: best for growing car and van businesses that sell through booking sites.
- **Rental Car Manager**: best for campervan and car rental that sells through agents.
- **TWICE Commerce (formerly Rentle)**: best for multi-store bike and sports shops that rent and resell.
- **RENTALL (formerly Navotar), TSD Rental and RentWorks Plus**: built for larger fleets; quote-based.

## RoveHQ

Built for small fleets, from one vehicle to a few dozen cars, scooters, motorbikes, quads or vans, and run from your phone. You set it up yourself in minutes, with no sales call or onboarding fee. A [free plan](/pricing) covers 2 cars and 5 motorbikes with no time limit; Starter (US$15, €13 or £11 a month) covers 5 cars and 10 motorbikes, Pro ($30, €27 or £22) 15 cars and 25 motorbikes, and Business (from $45, €40 or £33) any size, all charged in your currency. Cars and motorbikes are counted separately.

Customers can pay by card on your booking page, through embedded checkout or a payment link, with Apple Pay or Google Pay, or with SEPA Direct Debit, iDEAL, Bancontact, Klarna, MB Way or BLIK where Stripe offers them. You can hold a deposit on the card and release or capture it. Money goes to your own Stripe account. Payments through Stripe carry a RoveHQ platform fee (5% on Free, 2.5% Starter, 2% Pro, 1.5% Business, 1% on any yearly plan) on top of Stripe's fee. Cash, bank transfer and QR payments work too, with slip checking for PromptPay, QRIS and bank transfers.

It writes a rental agreement for each booking in English and the local language in 18 countries across Southeast Asia and Europe, signed on the customer's phone, reads passport and license photos, and records handovers with photos, a damage diagram and signature. LINE and Telegram chats sit in one inbox, with automatic reminders. The app and customer pages come in 28 languages, and RoveHQ imports your data from your current software for free.

**Not for you if** you need bookings from travel sites, telematics, a payment provider other than Stripe, or a large multi-branch operation.

## How to choose

1. **Count your vehicles, split into cars and two-wheelers.** Some tools charge per vehicle, some per plan band, some per location. A shop with 30 scooters can pay very different amounts.
2. **Work out the year-one cost,** including setup fees, add-ons, platform fees or commission on payments, and the payment provider's own fees, not just the monthly price.
3. **Match how customers pay.** Card online, a deposit held on the card, cash at the counter, bank transfer or a QR code. Check the tool handles the ones your customers use.
4. **Match how customers talk to you.** Email, LINE, WhatsApp or Telegram.
5. **Check the agreement language.** Can your customer read the contract they sign?
6. **Try it on your phone** with a real booking, handover and return.
7. **Check you can get your data out** if you leave.
