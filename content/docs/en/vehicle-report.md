Open it from **Reports**: under **Vehicle by vehicle**, tap the vehicle. Pick the period at the top the same way as on Reports.

## What's on it

- **Profit** for the period, and **Time rented** as a percentage.
- **Money in** (after refunds) with the number of rentals, and **Money out**, plus how much you spent and kept.
- **Average price per day**: what it actually earned per day out. If that's different from your listed price, you'll see "Your price is ..." under it.
- **Where the money went**: its costs split by type.
- **Rentals in this period**: a timeline and a list, with how many days it was out. Each rental shows the customer, dates and status, and anything still owed. Tap one to open the booking.
- **Month by month** (or **Week by week**).
- **Money in and out**: every entry for this vehicle in the period.

## What it cost and what it's worth

If you've entered the purchase price, you also see:

- **Bought for**
- **Worth now, roughly** and **Value lost since you bought it**, when it has a value
- **Profit in this period, as a share of its price**, so you can see how fast it's paying for itself

Tap **Change the price or value** to update them. See [Fleet value](/docs/fleet-value) for estimated values.
