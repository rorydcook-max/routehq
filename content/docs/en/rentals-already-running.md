For vehicles that are already out with a customer. No booking link needed.

## On the booking form

1. **Bookings** > **New booking** > **Already out**.
2. Choose the vehicle, then the customer (you need one here, because it's saved as on rent straight away).
3. Set the **Rental start date** to when it really started, even if that was months ago. Add the end date, or tick **Open-ended (no return date yet)**.
4. Check the rate and deposit. If they paid several months ahead, tick that under **More options**.
5. Tap **Continue**. There's no handover step.
6. Answer **What has the customer paid so far?**
   - **Up to date**: everything due so far is paid.
   - **Paid until a date**: anything due after that date shows as owed.
   - **Nothing paid yet**: everything due shows as owed.
7. If you already have their deposit, tick **You are holding the ... deposit**.
8. Tap **Save rental**.

The booking shows as **On rent**. Future rent appears on the **Payments** tab and your dashboard, and reminders go out if you've turned them on.

The handover form is optional for these. If you want a signed agreement, open the booking and tap **Create booking link** under **Customer's link**.

## Customer is in front of you now

Tick **Starting right now, and taking payment today**, enter what they paid on the last step and tap **Record walk-in rental**.

## Let the assistant do it

Faster if you have a few.

1. Open the quick menu and choose **Tell the assistant**, or tap **Assistant** on the **Fleet** page.
2. Tell it who has what. Type, talk, or paste a Google Sheet link. For example: "The white Yaris is with Anna Smith, +66 81 234 5678, since 3 March, 9,000 a month, next payment 3 November, 5,000 deposit."
3. It needs the name and phone, start date, price and whether per day/week/month, the next payment date (or return date) and any deposit.
4. Check the list and tap **Add ... rentals**. Nothing is saved before that.

Got them in a spreadsheet? See [Import a spreadsheet](/docs/import-a-spreadsheet).

## Common questions

**Does rent they already paid show as income?**
Yes. It goes into **Money in & out** on the date each payment was due. Months paid ahead (under **More options**) count on the start date.
