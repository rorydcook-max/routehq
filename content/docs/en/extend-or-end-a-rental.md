Most changes start from **Change terms** on the booking.

![A booking in RoveHQ: dates, billing, next payment, and buttons to start the return or change terms](/site/docs/screens/booking-page.webp)

## Extend a rental

1. Open the booking, tap **Change terms** > **Extend rental**.
2. Pick the new return date, or tap **+1 day**, **+3 days**, **+1 week** or **+1 month**.
3. RoveHQ prices the extra days from your rates. Tap **Use this**, or type the price you agreed. Set when it's due.
4. Leave **Customer signs the change (recommended)** ticked to send them a short form to sign, or untick it to just record it.
5. Tap **Create link for customer to sign** (or save if you unticked it).

No return date in mind? Tap **Make it monthly** to switch to monthly with no end.

**When the customer asks.** Customers can ask for more time from their booking page. You'll see it at the top of the booking: tap **Approve extension** or **Decline** (you can add a reason). If another booking is in the way, RoveHQ offers to move that booking to a free vehicle, or extend only as far as the vehicle is free. If you set **Notice needed to extend automatically** in Settings, requests made early enough are approved on their own.

## Bring the return date forward

Customer coming back early? **Change terms** > **Bring the return date forward** (or **Set the return date** on an open-ended rental). Pick the date and save. Rent due after that date comes off the schedule and the customer is told.

When the vehicle is back early, RoveHQ works out the unused days. On the **Vehicle & deposit** tab you'll see what they're worth by the day. Whether you refund it is up to you: tap **Refund ... or another amount**, or leave it. If the unused days aren't paid yet, choose **Take ... off** or **Keep as booked**. Refunds are decided by the owner or a manager.

## Change the rate or deposit

Once the customer has signed: **Change terms** > **Change rate or deposit**. Enter the new rate and from when, and/or the new deposit. A higher deposit is added as a payment due; a lower one is given back at the end. The customer gets a link and the change applies once they sign.

## Change the dates before it starts

If the vehicle hasn't gone out and the customer hasn't signed, **Change terms** opens **Change the dates**: the new dates replace the old ones and the rent is worked out again. Once they've signed, you get **Change the return date** instead, and they sign the change.

## Edit a booking

**More** > **Edit booking** lets you change the dates, price, deposit, handover place and time, what's included, and the payment list (edit, remove or **Add a payment**). Tap **Save changes**. To swap the vehicle, use **More** > **Change vehicle** instead.

## Cancel a booking

1. **More** > **Cancel booking**.
2. Tick why (customer canceled before handover, ended early, breakdown, canceled by us, other).
3. Choose how money is handled: full, part or no refund, and whether the deposit is returned or kept. This only updates your records. The money itself moves outside RoveHQ.
4. Choose what happens to the vehicle: **Available to rent**, **Send for repair**, or **Keep current status**.
5. Check **What will happen** and tap **Confirm cancellation**.

The customer's link stops working. Unpaid future payments are canceled.

For a test or duplicate booking, use **Delete booking** from the menu in the bookings list instead. Use **Cancel booking** for real ones.

## Common questions

**I canceled by mistake. Can I undo it?**
Yes. Open the booking and tap **Undo cancellation**. Refunds you recorded when canceling are not reversed.

**Why can't I edit the price on the edit page?**
Once the customer has signed, the terms are fixed. Use **Change terms** on the booking so they sign the change.
