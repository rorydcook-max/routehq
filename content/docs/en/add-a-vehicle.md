Go to **Fleet** and tap **Add vehicle** (or **+**, then **Add a vehicle**). Only the type, make and model are needed. Everything else can wait.

## From a photo of the blue book

1. Tap **Take or choose a photo** and take or pick a photo or PDF of the blue book (BPKB, OR/CR or your country's registration document).
2. RoveHQ fills in what it can read: plate, make, model, year, colour, frame number, engine, and tax and insurance dates.
3. Check every field before saving.

If it can't read it, fill it in by hand or tap **Use a different photo**. The photo is kept with the vehicle's papers.

## Make, model and plate

- **Type of vehicle**: Car, Van, Motorbike, Scooter, E-bike or ATV.
- **Make** and **Model**: pick from the list, or choose **Another make** / **Another model** and type it.
- **Year**, **Version**, **Number plate**, **Colour**.

**More about the vehicle** (frame number, gearbox, seats, engine) is optional.

## Rates and deposit

Fill in the ones you use out of **Daily rate**, **Weekly rate** and **Monthly rate**. A missing one is worked out from the others when you book.

Leave **Deposit for this vehicle** empty to use your usual deposit (set in **Settings**, **Rentals and payments**). See [Deposits](/docs/deposits).

## The extra sections

Tap to open any of these:

- **Tax, insurance and service dates**: road tax, compulsory insurance (Por Ror Bor), your own insurance and cover type, next service and oil change. You get reminded before they run out. See [Paperwork and renewals](/docs/paperwork-and-renewals).
- **Mileage and value**: mileage now and when bought, purchase price, worth now. Only the owner sees price and value.
- **Location**: only if you have more than one location.
- **Loan**: sale price, down payment, monthly payment, loan length and months left. RoveHQ shows the interest and what's still to pay. See [Vehicle loans](/docs/vehicle-loans).

Tap **Save vehicle**. Add photos and anything you skipped from the [vehicle page](/docs/vehicle-page).

During setup you get a shorter form with **Photos** (up to 8; the first is the main photo) and prices. The rest can be added later from **Fleet**.

## Common questions

**Do I have to set a price?**
No, but a vehicle with no price doesn't show on your online booking page, and you'll be asked for a price when you book it.
