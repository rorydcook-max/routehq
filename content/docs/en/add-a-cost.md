## From a receipt photo (fastest)

1. In **Money in & out**, tap **Add a cost from a receipt photo**.
2. Tap **Read receipt** and take or choose a photo (a screenshot or PDF works too).
3. RoveHQ fills in the amount, date, type of cost, who you paid, the mileage and a short note. If the plate is printed on the receipt, it picks the vehicle too.
4. Check it, fix anything wrong, tap **Save**. The photo is kept as the receipt.

If it can't read the photo, tap **Another photo** or type it in. If you've saved the same receipt before, RoveHQ warns you so you don't count it twice.

## Not sure where a receipt goes?

Tap **Upload a receipt** from the + button, choose the photo, then under **Where does it go?** pick:

- **A new entry**: a new cost filled in from the receipt
- **An entry already recorded**: adds the photo to a cost from the last 3 months that has no receipt
- **A payment you're waiting for**: marks a customer payment as paid, with the photo

## By hand

1. Tap **Record money** (on a phone: + button, **Record money in or out**), choose **Money out**.
2. Pick **What was it for?** and the **Vehicle**.
3. Enter the amount and date. Tap **Who you paid and the mileage (optional)** if you want them.
4. Tap **Save**.

## Shop rent, phone, ads, wages

Under **Vehicle**, choose **No vehicle (business cost)**. It counts in your totals and profit but not against any car. It shows as **Business cost** in the list.

## Servicing and repairs

For **Servicing**, **Maintenance** or **Repair** on a vehicle, a **Service details** box opens. Enter the mileage, tick **Oil changed** if it was (not shown for a repair), and set **Next service due (date)**, **(km)** or both. The work goes into the vehicle's service history. See [Service and maintenance](/docs/service-and-maintenance).

## Bill the renter

For damage, a fine or fuel the renter should pay, tick **Charge this to the customer** (it shows when the vehicle has bookings). From a booking, **More** > **Add a cost** opens the form with it already ticked.

1. Check the **Booking to bill**. RoveHQ picks whoever had the car that day.
2. Check the **Amount to bill**.
3. It comes out of the deposit first. If the bill is bigger than the deposit, the rest is billed to the customer. Untick that if insurance covers it.
4. Tap **Save**.

The cost stays as money out. The customer is told and can pay from their booking page. If they never pay, choose **Won't be paid** on the booking. More in [Charge a renter](/docs/charge-a-renter).
