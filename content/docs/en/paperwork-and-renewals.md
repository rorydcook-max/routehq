Open the vehicle, go to **Paperwork & service**, and open **Tax, insurance and service dates**.

## What's on the list

It follows your business's country:

- **Thailand**: Road tax, Compulsory insurance (Por Ror Bor), Insurance.
- **Malaysia, Indonesia, Philippines, Vietnam and others**: road tax or registration, compulsory insurance, insurance and vehicle inspection, with the local names in brackets (for example PUSPAKOM, KIR, CTPL).

Plus **Next service**, **Oil change**, and **Loan ends** if there's a loan. Each shows when it runs out and the days left: amber at 30 days, red at 7.

## Add a date

Tap **Add the date**, fill in **Runs out on** and the cost, and tap **Save**. To do them all at once, use **Edit** on the vehicle.

## Record a renewal

1. Tap **Renewed it? Enter the new date**.
2. Add a photo of the new certificate, policy or receipt under **Photo of the document (optional)**. RoveHQ fills in the dates and cost from it. Check the date.
3. Check **Cover starts** and **Runs out on**.
4. Under **How is it paid?** pick **All at once** or **Every month**, and enter the cost.
5. Tap **Save**.

The cost goes into your costs for that vehicle. A yearly payment is counted a share each month. Monthly payments are added each month until the cover ends. The photo is kept in the vehicle's files.

You can also record the cost from **Money in & out** and let it update the date. See [Insurance and tax costs](/docs/insurance-and-tax-costs).

## Renewal help in Thailand

With 90 days or fewer left, road tax, Por Ror Bor and insurance get a **How to renew** box (for Thai businesses, or if you haven't set a country):

- Road tax: online renewal, or an inspection (ตรอ.) first for cars over 7 years and bikes over 5. Renew Por Ror Bor first if it has 90 days or less, because online tax needs more than that. There's a **DLT e-service** link.
- Por Ror Bor: tell the seller it's a rental vehicle.
- Insurance: check it covers rental use and get two or three quotes for the same cover.

**Copy vehicle details** (**Copy details for a quote** for Por Ror Bor and insurance) copies the plate, model, year and VIN to send to an insurer. It's general guidance, not legal advice.

## Reminders

- The vehicle's **Paperwork & service** tab is flagged and opens first at 7 days.
- **Fleet** shows it and counts it under **need attention**.
- The dashboard lists anything due within 7 days in **Today**.
- The LINE morning summary includes road tax, Por Ror Bor, insurance, inspection and service due within 7 days or already overdue, plus a heads-up 90, 60 and 30 days before any paperwork runs out. See [Notifications](/docs/notifications).

## Common questions

**I pay my insurance monthly. How do I record that?**
Choose **Every month** under **How is it paid?** and enter the **Cost per month**. RoveHQ adds it to your costs each month until the cover ends.

**Where's the inspection for my older Thai car?**
In Thailand it's done with the road tax, so it has no date of its own. When you renew road tax on a car over 7 years or a bike over 5, the form asks for the inspection cost.
