Anolla says you can export raw data at any time to Excel, Google Sheets or BI tools. We couldn't find a help article that lists the columns, so RoveHQ reads the file the usual way: it shows you what each column was read as, and you correct anything before it's saved.

## Free import

Importing is free on every plan. RoveHQ reads any Excel or CSV file and Google Sheets links. You see every vehicle, booking and payment before anything is saved, and you can take the whole import back within 30 days. If you'd rather not do it yourself, [send us the files](/contact) and we'll bring them in for you.
