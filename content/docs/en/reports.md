Pick a period at the top: **This month**, **Last month**, **Last 3 months**, **Last 6 months**, **This year**, **Last year**, or **Choose dates** (then **Show these dates**). Everything on the page follows it.

## What you see

- **Profit**, **Money in** and **Money out**, each compared with the period before ("Up 12% on the period before"), plus how much you spent and kept. Yearly costs like insurance count as a share each month.
- **Who owes you money**: each customer, how much and since when. Tap one to open them.
- **What stands out**: tap **Show me** for a short read of your figures and ideas on what to do.
- **Deposits**: **Holding now**, **Taken in this period**, **Given back**, and what you kept for damage or unpaid rent (that part counts as income).
- **Month by month** (or **Week by week** for short periods), with your best month.
- **Where the money came from** and **Where the money went**, split by type. Refunds show as **Given back to customers**.

## Vehicle by vehicle

Each vehicle's profit and **% rented** for the period.

- **Sort by** profit, money in, money out, rented most, rentals or earned back.
- Switch **List** / **Tiles**.
- Tap a vehicle for its full report, including its average price per day. See [Vehicle report](/docs/vehicle-report).
- Costs with no vehicle show underneath as **Business costs**.
- Tap **Compare**, then 2 to 4 vehicles to see them side by side. **Done** closes it.

**% rented** is the share of days the vehicle was out. It counts bookings plus rented spells from before your bookings were in RoveHQ. Overlapping days count once.

## More

Links at the bottom go to [Fleet value](/docs/fleet-value), [Owners' statements](/docs/owner-statements) and all your money in and out.

**Take a copy** downloads a **PDF** or **Spreadsheet** for your accountant (in English).

## Common questions

**Why is % rented high for a car with few bookings in RoveHQ?**
It also counts rented spells from before your bookings were in RoveHQ, if your history was brought in. Overlapping days count once.
