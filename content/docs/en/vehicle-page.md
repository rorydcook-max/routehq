Open **Fleet** and tap a vehicle.

## The fleet list

Vehicles are grouped by type. The counts at the top (**on rent**, **available**, **need attention**) are filters: tap one to see just those, tap again to see all.

Each vehicle shows **On rent**, **Booked**, **In the shop**, **Available** or **Free until** a date.

On a computer, the list also lets you tap **Set price** when there's no price yet, or **Add dates** when there are no tax or insurance dates. The owner also sees each vehicle's profit, the **Import** button, and tick boxes to **Archive** or **Delete** several at once (delete asks again and can't be undone). Staff don't get these.

## Top of the page

Photo, status, name, plate and prices. The main button follows what the vehicle is doing: **New booking** when it's free, **Record money** when it's out. **More for this vehicle** has **Log a service or repair**, **Condition report**, **Record a fine** and **Put in the shop**.

If it's rented or booked, a card shows the customer, dates, deposit held and what's **Due now**, with **Open booking**, **Start handover** or **Start return**, and **Extend or end early**.

Below that are four tiles (profit, how often it's rented, next paperwork, mileage) and **The year at a glance**: rentals on the top line, services, checks and paperwork dates on the bottom. Tap a rental bar to open the booking.

## The tabs

- **To do**: jobs for this vehicle. **Add a job** to add one.
- **Money**: money in and costs, and every payment for this vehicle.
- **Paperwork & service**: tax, insurance and service dates, services and repairs, files, and the loan.
- **Rentals**: how often it's rented and past rentals.
- **Checks**: handover, return and condition reports.
- **Details**: make, model, VIN, rates, deposit, tracker and **Notes**.
- **History**: everything that happened to it.

## Photos

Tap the photo (or **Add photos**), then **Choose photos**. Drag a photo by its arrows to change the order. The first one is the **Main photo**.

## Edit

Tap **Edit** to change rates, plate, mileage, condition, dates and specs, then **Save changes**. Owners also see purchase price, value, owner's share and loan. To stop renting it, use **Archive vehicle** at the bottom. Its history stays.

## Put it in the shop

**More for this vehicle** > **Put in the shop**. When it's fixed, tap **Back and free to rent** at the top. Only works when the vehicle is free.

## Notes

**Details** tab > **Notes**. Type anything, like where the spare key is. It saves when you tap away.

## Common questions

**Why does the page open on a different tab each time?**
It opens what needs you first: **Paperwork & service** if something runs out within 7 days, then **To do** if there are open jobs. Otherwise owners get **Money** and staff get **Rentals**.

**Can my staff see what I paid for the vehicle?**
No. What you paid, what it's worth and profit are only shown to the owner.
