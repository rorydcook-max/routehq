If you already have a contract you've used for years, you can keep using it. Upload it and RoveHQ turns it into a copy customers can sign online, with your logo, their details filled in and both signatures at the end.

## Upload it

1. **Settings** > **Business** > **Rental agreement**.
2. Under **Use your own agreement**, choose a PDF, a Word file (.docx) or photos of the pages (up to 10).
3. Tap **Upload and read it**. Reading takes a minute or two.

Photos work best taken flat, in good light, one page per photo.

Sent your contract in the chat instead? Tap **Upload my own contract** there and it opens here and starts reading.

## Check it

You'll see your original on one side and the signable copy on the other, filled in with a sample booking.

- **Blanks filled from each booking**: each blank in your contract shows as a chip. Pick what goes in it (renter's name, plate, start date, deposit...), choose **A line to fill in by hand**, or tap **×** to put your original text back. A detail the booking doesn't have yet (a return time, say) prints as a blank line.
- **Your wording**: fix anything that was read wrong. Leave an empty line between clauses.
- **Add the booking details at the top**: tick this if your contract doesn't already ask for the renter and vehicle.

Tap **Save changes** as you go.

## Thailand: what the rules expect

If your business is in Thailand, RoveHQ checks your contract against the 2025 rental-contract rules (two copies, deposit refunds, replacement vehicle and so on). Anything missing shows as a warning. Tap **Add this clause** to add RoveHQ's wording, in English and Thai. They're warnings, not blockers: it's your call.

## Start using it

Tap **Use this agreement for new bookings**. From then on every new booking uses your agreement. Customers sign it the same way, and you countersign with your saved signature. **Your own terms** are still added at the end; the numbers under **Change these numbers** apply only to the RoveHQ agreement.

Upload a newer version later and it waits as a draft until you choose it, so what's in use never changes by surprise.

Changed your mind? Tap **Go back to the RoveHQ agreement**. Your upload is kept, so you can switch again later.
