Everything is in **Settings** > **Notifications**: alerts and the morning summary, by push to your devices and by email.

## Choose what you hear about

The **Alerts and morning summary** table has a row for each alert and a column for **Push** and **Email**.

1. Tick the boxes you want. The alerts are **Booking signed**, **Payment**, **Customer request**, **New message**, **Vehicle returned**, **Your RoveHQ plan** and the **Morning summary (8:00 am)**.
2. Tap **Save**.

A column marked **Coming soon** isn't ready on your account yet.

## Turn on push on each device

1. Under **Alerts on this device**, tap **Enable alerts on this device** and allow it.
2. Tap **Send a test** to check.

Do this on each phone or computer you want alerts on. Push goes to every device where someone turned it on. On iPhone, push only works after you add RoveHQ to your Home Screen.

## Email

Under **Email**, alerts and the summary go to the address in **Send to**.

## Morning summary on LINE

For businesses in Thailand. Optional: get the morning summary in your own LINE too.

1. Tap **Add friend in LINE** and add the RoveHQ account.
2. Tap **Get a connection code**, then **Send in LINE**. The code is filled in; just send it. It works for 30 minutes.
3. The card shows **Connected** within a few seconds.

Around 8:00 each morning (Thailand time) you get today's handovers and returns, what's overdue and what's owed. Switch **Morning summary** off and tap **Save LINE settings** to pause it. **Send a test summary** sends today's summary now.

LINE and Telegram no longer send other alerts. To read and answer customer chats from your LINE Official Account or Telegram bot, connect them in **Settings** > **Messaging**. See [Inbox and messaging](/docs/inbox-and-messaging).

## Calendar feed

To see handovers and returns in Google, Apple or Outlook calendar, go to **Settings** > **Integrations** > **Calendar feed**. See [Calendar](/docs/calendar).

## Common questions

**Alerts don't work on my iPhone.**
On iPhone, push only works once RoveHQ is on your Home Screen. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, open RoveHQ from there and enable alerts again.

**Where did Telegram and LINE alerts go?**
Alerts now come by push and email only. In Thailand you can still get the morning summary on LINE. To chat with customers on LINE or Telegram, connect them in **Settings** > **Messaging**.
