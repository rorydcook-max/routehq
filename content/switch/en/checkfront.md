RoveHQ recognises Checkfront's booking export by its headings (booking, status, customer name and email, start and end dates, total and paid). Cancelled, void and on-hold bookings are listed but left out unless you tick them.

Ticking **Use ISO dates** in Checkfront means dates can't be misread. Without it, RoveHQ works out the date order from the file, and asks you when it can't tell.

## Free import

Importing is free on every plan. RoveHQ recognises Checkfront exports and reads them straight away. You see every vehicle, booking and payment before anything is saved, and you can take the whole import back within 30 days.
