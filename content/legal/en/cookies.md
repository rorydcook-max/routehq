> Written by RoveHQ from public guidance (ICO, EDPB, European Commission, Thai PDPC); not reviewed by a lawyer.

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. Similar browser storage ("local storage") works the same way, so we treat it the same.

## Your choice

On our website (rovehq.io) and in the RoveHQ app (rovehq.app) we ask before using any cookie that isn't strictly necessary. **Accept** and **Reject** are equally easy, and nothing is hidden if you reject. You can change your mind at any time with **Cookie choices**: at the bottom of every page on rovehq.io, and under Privacy in **My account** on rovehq.app.

We remember your choice for 6 months in your browser (`rovehq_consent`), then ask again. rovehq.io and rovehq.app are separate websites, so each one asks and remembers its own choice.

## Strictly necessary (no consent needed)

These make the site and app work. Under the UK PECR, the EU ePrivacy Directive and Thailand's PDPA they don't need consent.

| Name | What it does | How long |
|---|---|---|
| `rovehq_consent` (local storage and cookie) | Remembers your cookie choice | 6 months |
| `routehq_locale` | Remembers the language you picked | 1 year |
| `routehq_theme` | Remembers light or dark mode | 1 year |
| `sb-…-auth-token` | Keeps you signed in to RoveHQ | Until you sign out (refreshed while in use) |
| `rhq_active_org` | Remembers which business you're working in | While you use the app |
| `rhq_gate` | Short security check that you're allowed into a page | 5 minutes |
| `routehq_customer_locale` | The language a renter picked on a booking page | 1 year |
| `rove_visitor` and chat thread (local storage) | Keeps your chat with our team together, only once you open the chat | 1 year |
| App preferences (local storage: calendar view, dismissed tips, unsent inspection drafts) | Remembers how you like the app and saves work in progress | Until cleared |

## Analytics (only with your consent)

| Name | Company | What it does | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_ga`, `_ga_<id>` | Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Ltd / Google LLC) | Counts visits, which pages are used, and how many people sign up or sign in (Google Analytics 4 doesn't store IP addresses) | Up to 14 months |
| `rovehq_event` | RoveHQ | Tells the next page that you just signed up or signed in, so it's counted once (no details about you) | Up to 30 minutes, deleted once read |

We use Google Analytics 4, loaded through Google Tag Manager (which sets no cookies of its own), on rovehq.io and rovehq.app, and only after you click **Accept**. Until then neither is loaded at all. If you reject or later withdraw, Google is told straight away, it stops storing or sending anything, and we delete the `_ga` cookies we can reach. Nothing we send to Google identifies you: no name, email address, phone number, business name or account ID.

## Things we don't do

- No advertising or tracking cookies, and no selling of data. Google's advertising features stay switched off.
- Businesses' booking pages (their own addresses, rovehq.app/rent, rove.rent/<business> and booking links) and the booking box on their websites set only strictly necessary cookies. We never run analytics on them: renters' details belong to the business.
- rove.rent's search page asks the same question and, only if you accept, measures visits with its own Google Analytics 4 property (the same `_ga` cookies, up to 14 months).

## Other companies' content

Booking pages use Google Maps for address search. Google receives your IP address when the map loads, as it does for any website that shows a Google map; RoveHQ doesn't let Google set advertising cookies through it. If you message a business through LINE, Telegram or WhatsApp, those apps' own policies apply.
