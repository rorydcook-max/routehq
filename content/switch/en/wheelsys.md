Wheelsys (Wheels Car Rental System) is car rental software from Athens. It says its reports can be exported in many formats, printed, emailed or delivered to your inbox on a schedule. It doesn't publish the column names, so RoveHQ reads the files the usual way: it shows you what each column was read as, and you correct anything before it's saved.

## Free import

Importing is free on every plan. RoveHQ reads any Excel or CSV file. You see every vehicle, booking and payment before anything is saved, and you can take the whole import back within 30 days. If you'd rather not do it yourself, [send us the files](/contact) and we'll bring them in for you.
