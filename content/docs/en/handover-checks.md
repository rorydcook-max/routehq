Do this every time. If there's ever an argument about a scratch or the fuel, the photos and signature settle it.

## Hand a vehicle over

1. Open the booking and tap **Start handover**. Or open the quick menu, tap **Hand over a vehicle** and pick the booking.
2. Go through the steps. Tap **Next** after each one.
   - **Odometer**: take a photo. RoveHQ reads the number from it. Check it and fix it if it's wrong.
   - **Fuel**: photo of the gauge, then tap the level that matches.
   - **Walkaround**: one video walking round the vehicle, or a photo of each of the four sides.
   - **Damage**: tap the part of the vehicle where there's damage, choose the type (scratch, dent, crack, missing part), write a few words and tap **Add this damage**. Then tap **Add damage photo** on it. Nothing there? Tick **No damage to report**.
   - **GPS**: confirm the tracker is working. Skipped if the vehicle has no tracker.
   - **Signature**: the customer types their name and signs on your phone.
3. On **Check and sign**, if they pay cash now, enter the rent and deposit and tap **Confirm cash and make receipt**. You can share or download the receipt.
4. Tap **Finish handover**.

If the customer hasn't signed the agreement yet, RoveHQ warns you. Get their signature before you give them the keys.

Going out early or late? RoveHQ asks whether to charge for the extra days or when rent should start.

## Take a vehicle back

1. Open the booking and tap **Start return**, or use **Take a vehicle back** in the quick menu.
2. Do the same steps. RoveHQ shows what it was like at handover, so you can compare:
   - km driven since handover,
   - fuel at handover, and roughly how much less there is now,
   - damage that was **Already on the vehicle at handover**, so you only add new damage.
3. If fuel is missing or there's new damage, you can type what you're charging.
4. On the **Deposit** step, settle the deposit (see [Deposits](/docs/deposits)).
5. The customer signs and you tap **Finish return**.

If they still owe rent, RoveHQ shows it. **Collect now**, or leave it on the booking to chase later.

## Condition check without a booking

To record a vehicle's condition any time (after a repair, before selling), open the vehicle and tap **Condition report**. Same steps, then **Save condition report**.

## See a finished check

On the booking, under **Handover and return**, tap **Show the completed form** to see the photos, fuel, damage and signature.

## Common questions

**Can I hand over without the form?**
Yes, from the booking tap **Hand over without the form**. But you'll have no photos or signature if there's a dispute later.
