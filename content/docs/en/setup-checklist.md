## Step 1: Phone and location

1. Choose your **Language**.
2. Add a **Phone number customers can call** (optional; it goes on agreements and your booking page).
3. Check **Country** and type your **Town or area**.
4. Tap **Continue**.

Your country sets the currency for your prices.

## Step 2: Add your vehicles

Pick one:

- **Tell our assistant**: type, talk or send photos. See [The fleet assistant](/docs/fleet-assistant).
- **Fill in a short form**: one vehicle at a time. See [Add a vehicle](/docs/add-a-vehicle).
- **Bring in a list**: from a spreadsheet. See [Import a spreadsheet](/docs/import-a-spreadsheet).
- **Send us your records, we'll set it up**: tap **Add photos or files** (notebook photos, spreadsheet, chat exports), add a note in **Anything we should know?**, then **Send to RoveHQ**. We usually have it in within one working day.

Then tap **Go to my dashboard**, or **I'll add vehicles later**.

## The checklist

The owner's **Dashboard** then shows **Get ready for your first booking**, three steps at a time. Steps tick off by themselves. Tap one to go there.

- **Add your vehicles**
- **Add who's renting now**: opens the assistant. See [Rentals already running](/docs/rentals-already-running).
- **Turn on automatic reminders**: done once a chat app is connected in **Settings**, **Messaging**. See [Automatic customer messages](/docs/automatic-customer-messages).
- **Share your booking page**: tap to copy or share the link. Put it on Facebook, Google Maps and LINE.
- **Add your signature**: customers can't finish an online booking without it.
- **Say how customers pay you**: add PromptPay, bank, Wise or Revolut. See [Payment reminders and methods](/docs/payment-reminders-and-methods).
- **Verify your email**: only if you haven't yet. Tap it for a new link.
- **Take your first booking**. See [Create a booking](/docs/create-a-booking).
- **Read the rental agreement**. See [Rental agreements](/docs/rental-agreements).
- **Get the morning summary on LINE**: connects it in **Settings**, **Notifications** (businesses in Thailand). See [Alerts and the morning summary](/docs/notifications).
- **Invite a team member**. See [Invite your team](/docs/invite-your-team).

To hide it, tap the **X** on the card.

## Common questions

**Where did the checklist go?**
It shows until your first booking or rental is in RoveHQ, or until you hide it. Once hidden it doesn't come back, but every step can still be done from Fleet, Settings and so on.
