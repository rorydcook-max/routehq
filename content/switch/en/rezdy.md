RoveHQ recognizes Rezdy's orders export by its order number, product name and customer columns. In Rezdy a deposit can mean a part payment, so that column is not read as a security deposit held: what was paid comes from the paid amount.

## Free import

Importing is free on every plan. RoveHQ recognizes Rezdy exports and reads them straight away. You see every vehicle, booking and payment before anything is saved, and you can take the whole import back within 30 days. If you'd rather not do it yourself, [send us the files](/contact) and we'll bring them in for you.
