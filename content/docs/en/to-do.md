**To do** lists payments to collect and jobs, grouped by **Overdue**, **Next 3 days**, **Next 7 days** and **Next 30 days**. Jobs further off or with no date are under **Later**. The counts at the top are filters: tap one to see just those.

![RoveHQ To do list with a refund to decide and next month's rent to collect](/site/docs/screens/to-do.webp)

## What shows up

Most of it is added for you:

- rent, deposits, extensions, charges and extras on the day they're due
- receipts customers send from their booking page
- customer requests, like keeping the vehicle longer or a problem with it
- booking jobs: a vehicle not handed over, a refund to decide, a deposit to settle
- jobs you add on a vehicle's page

Tap the customer or vehicle on a job to open the booking or vehicle.

## Add a job

1. Open the vehicle and go to its **To do** tab.
2. Tap **Add a job**.
3. Fill in **What needs doing**, a **Due date** and the **Kind of job**.
4. Tap **Add a job**.

## Record a payment

1. Tap **Record payment**.
2. Enter the **Amount that arrived**, **Date received** and **Method**.
3. If they paid for more than one thing, tick it under **What is it for?**. **What will be recorded** shows what's left or extra.
4. Leave **Tell the customer the payment was received** ticked if you want them told.
5. Tap **Record** (the button shows the amount).

## Check a customer's receipt

RoveHQ reads the slip and tells you if the amount matches. Tap **View receipt**, then **Confirm received**. If it's different, tap **Something's different** and enter what arrived, or **Nothing arrived**.

## Jobs with their own button

Some jobs take you to the next step and close when it's done: **Hand over**, **Answer**, **Record refund** or **No refund**, **Settle deposit**, **Give them free** or **Keep the charge**, **Open form**.

Refunds and free days are for the owner or a manager. Staff see "The owner or a manager decides this one."

## Mark it done

Tap **Mark done**. To add a note, tap the three dots, **Add note**, then **Save & mark done**. Finished jobs are under **Done**.

## Common questions

**How do I add a job?**
Open the vehicle, go to its **To do** tab and tap **Add a job**. Payments and booking jobs are added for you.
