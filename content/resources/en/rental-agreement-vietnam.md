Self-drive rental in Vietnam runs on two very different customers: backpackers renting a semi-automatic bike for a few weeks, and Vietnamese families renting a seven-seat car for Tết or a weekend in Đà Lạt. Both are now covered by a new traffic law, [Law 36/2024 on Road Traffic Order and Safety](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Giao-thong-Van-tai/Law-36-2024-QH15-Road-Traffic-Order-and-Safety-620124.aspx), in force since 1 January 2025, and a new personal data law in force since 1 January 2026. Many shop contracts predate both.

This version of our free agreement is built on the current rules. Vietnam is preset in the form; add your details and download.

## What the Vietnam version covers

**Licences.** Law 36/2024 [sets the classes](https://hethongphapluat.com/luat-trat-tu-an-toan-giao-thong-duong-bo-2024/dieu-57): A1 for motorbikes up to 125 cc or 11 kW, A above that, B for cars up to 8 passenger seats. The minimum age is 18. Licences issued before 2025 stay valid for the vehicles printed on them.

**Foreign renters and the IDP.** Vietnam acceded to the 1968 Vienna Convention, and an IDP from a Convention country is [valid only together with the matching national licence](https://daibieunhandan.vn/print/10330947.html). [Circular 12/2025/TT-BCA](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/toan-van-thong-tu-12-2025-tt-bca-cua-bo-cong-an-quy-dinh-ve-sat-hach-cap-giay-phep-lai-xe-119250303174347028.htm) says treaty IDPs are used as the treaty provides. So the clause accepts a foreign licence plus a 1968-type IDP, or a converted Vietnamese licence, and warns that permits under other conventions may not be accepted. That matters because some countries issue 1949-type permits. Handing a vehicle to someone not qualified to drive it is itself [prohibited](https://hethongphapluat.com/luat-trat-tu-an-toan-giao-thong-duong-bo-2024/dieu-9), so the agreement says you won't.

**Zero alcohol.** The same article bans driving with any alcohol in the blood or breath. The agreement repeats it, and breaking it removes insurance protection.

**Helmets and passengers.** Rider and passenger must wear a [national-standard helmet with the strap fastened](https://hethongphapluat.com/luat-trat-tu-an-toan-giao-thong-duong-bo-2024/dieu-33), with one passenger (two only in listed cases, such as a child under 12).

**Seatbelts and child seats.** All belted seats need belts worn, and from 1 January 2026 a child under 10 and under 1.35 m [must not sit in the driver's row and needs a child safety device](https://luatvietnam.vn/giao-thong/lap-ghe-an-toan-cho-tre-em-tren-o-to-863-105344-article.html). Worth offering child seats as an extra.

**Documents to carry.** The driver must carry the registration certificate (or a permitted copy), licence, inspection certificate where needed and compulsory insurance certificate ([Art. 56](https://hethongphapluat.com/luat-trat-tu-an-toan-giao-thong-duong-bo-2024/dieu-56)). A Vietnam-only clause lists what you hand over and makes the renter return it.

**Compulsory insurance.** Owner's civil liability insurance under Decree 67/2023 covers third parties and passengers, with limits of [VND 150 million per person for injury or death](https://lsvn.vn/thiet-hai-nao-se-duoc-doanh-nghiep-bao-hiem-xe-co-gioi-boi-thuong-1697152694-a136310.html) and VND 50 million (motorbikes) or 100 million (cars) for property. It never covers the rented vehicle, which needs voluntary physical damage insurance (bảo hiểm vật chất xe).

**Passports and ID.** Holding a passport as security is the classic Vietnamese rental habit. It is also illegal: pledging or accepting passports, and unlawfully holding another person's passport, is fined under [Decree 282/2025](https://luatvietnam.vn/an-ninh-trat-tu/nghi-dinh-282-2025-nd-cp-xu-phat-vi-pham-hanh-chinh-trong-an-ninh-trat-tu-an-toan-xa-hoi-417708-d1.html), and the [Law on Identification 2023](https://lsvn.vn/phat-tien-co-so-luu-tru-giu-can-cuoc-cua-khach-a167113.html) bans the same for ID cards. The agreement uses a cash deposit (ký cược under the Civil Code, Art. 329) and a photo of the document instead.

**Personal data.** The [Personal Data Protection Law 91/2025](https://luatvietnam.vn/tin-van-ban-moi/da-co-luat-bao-ve-du-lieu-ca-nhan-2025-so-91-2025-qh15-186-102925-article.html) and [Decree 356/2025](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Quyen-dan-su/Nghi-dinh-356-2025-ND-CP-huong-dan-Luat-Bao-ve-du-lieu-ca-nhan-687428.aspx) apply from 2026, with some duties relaxed for household businesses and micro-enterprises. The data clause covers purpose, retention, tracker location data and the renter's consent.

**Camera fines.** Phạt nguội notices reach the owner weeks later. Provincial guidance says the owner [can identify the renter with the contract, ID and licence](https://hatinh.gov.vn/vi/bai-viet/xu-ly-the-nao-voi-tai-xe-thue-xe-tu-lai-khi-bi-phat-nguoi) so the renter is fined. The agreement makes the renter cooperate and repay you if you are fined, and lets you hold part of the deposit for 30 days.

**Language and disputes.** English and Vietnamese side by side, Vietnamese prevailing, disputes before the competent People's Court.

## Common problems it handles

- A northern loop that ends in Hà Giang with a broken bike: the renter must call you before any repair, small emergency repairs up to your limit are refunded, and faults caused by misuse (ignoring a warning light, riding off-road) are the renter's cost. You can also set a permitted area.
- Late returns: under [Civil Code Art. 482](https://vbpl.vn/van-ban/chi-tiet/bo-luat-dan-su-so-91-2015-qh13--95942) the renter bears the risk during a late return, and the agreement says so.
- Scratches and missing mirrors, settled with Annex A photos. See our [handover checklist guide](/guides/vehicle-handover-checklist).
