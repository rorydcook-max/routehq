**My account** is at the bottom of the menu (on a phone, under **More**). Everyone on the team has one, including staff who can't open **Settings**. Nothing here changes the business or anyone else's view.

## Your sign-in

Shows your **Email** and **Role** (Owner, Manager or Teammate). Tap **Change password** to get a reset link by email. **Sign out** is here too.

## Language and dates

- **Language**: the language you see RoveHQ in. Customers' messages and agreements use the language set on each customer.
- **Years when you type a date**: **Western years (2026)** or **Thai Buddhist years (2569)**. It's used when you type dates like tax and insurance expiry.

Tap **Save**.

## Light or dark

Pick **Same as device**, **Light** or **Dark**. It applies to this device only and saves straight away.

## Alerts on this device

Tap **Enable alerts on this device** and allow it, then **Send a test**. Do it on each phone or computer you use. On an iPhone, add RoveHQ to your home screen first. What you're alerted about is set in **Settings**, **Integrations**. See [Alerts](/docs/notifications).

## Common questions

**Will changing my language change it for my team?**
No. Everything on **My account** is yours alone. Everyone else keeps their own choice.
