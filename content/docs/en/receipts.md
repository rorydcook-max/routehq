When a customer pays you cash at handover or return, RoveHQ makes them a PDF receipt. It has your logo (or business name), a receipt number, the date, the amount marked PAID, how they paid, the customer, the vehicle and plate, the rental dates and your footer line. It's in English and Thai.

## At handover

1. During the handover check, find the **Cash payment** box.
2. Enter the **Rental payment received**, and the **Deposit amount received** if there is one.
3. Tap **Confirm cash and make receipt**.
4. When it says **Receipt ready**, tap **Share receipt** or **Download receipt**.

The money is recorded at the same time, rent in **Money in & out** and the deposit as held, so don't record it again.

Already paid? The handover says **Already paid. Nothing to collect at handover.** Tap **Record cash anyway** if they hand you cash.

## At the return

If money is still owed, you'll see **Still owed on this booking**. Tap **Collect now**, enter what you got and tap **Confirm cash and make receipt**.

## Sharing

On a phone, **Share receipt** opens your share menu (LINE, WhatsApp, email…). On a computer it copies the link.

The customer can also open it any time from their booking page. You'll find all your receipts in **Documents** under the **Receipts** filter.

## Receipt number and footer

1. **Settings** > **Rentals and payments** > **How customers pay you**, scroll to **Receipts**.
2. **Receipt numbers start with**: up to 6 letters, like your initials. Numbers look like REC-2026-0001 and start again at 0001 each year. Left empty, it uses REC.
3. **Line at the bottom of each receipt**: something like "Thanks for renting with us. Drive safe."
4. Tap **Save**.

Your logo comes from your business details in Settings.
