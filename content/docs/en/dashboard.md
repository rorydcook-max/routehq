Almost everything on the dashboard can be tapped to open the details.

## The counts at the top

**overdue** and **for today** are jobs on [To do](/docs/to-do). **going out** and **coming back** are today's handovers and returns, and open the [Calendar](/docs/calendar). On a computer, **New booking** is on the right.

New businesses see their setup steps here first.

## Today

What needs you now, most urgent first, each with one button:

- **Return late**: tap **Check in** when it's back. It warns you if someone has it booked next.
- **Hand over** or **coming back** today.
- **Link not filled in**: a booking starts soon and the customer hasn't filled in their details.
- Money **overdue** or **due today**: tap **Record payment**.
- **Receipt to check**: a customer says they've paid.
- A customer **is waiting** for an answer, or reported a problem: tap **Answer**.
- A refund or free days to decide: tap **Decide**.
- Paperwork or a service due within 7 days: tap **Update** (three or more are grouped into one row with **Review**).
- Messages to send to customers who haven't opened a chat with you: tap **Send**.

Nothing to do? It says "All clear".

## The rest of the page

- **What's on**: the week's handovers, returns, payments, renewals and jobs.
- **Your fleet**: every vehicle and whether it's late, coming back, on rent, going out, free or not available. Tap one to open it.
- **Messages**: your latest three chats. If no chat app is connected, tap **Connect**. See [Inbox and messaging](/docs/inbox-and-messaging).
- **Bookings**: how many are **on rent**, **start this week** and are **unpaid**.

## The tiles

- **Overdue** (or **Due today**, or **All paid**): what customers owe you.
- The next vehicle going out or coming back.
- **Messages**: written for you, ready to send.
- **Deposits held**: customers' money, not income.
- **November so far** (named after the current month): this month's profit compared with this time last month, money in and out, and a chart by week. Owner only. Tap it for [Reports](/docs/reports).

## Common questions

**Why can't my staff see the profit?**
The month's profit is for the owner only. Staff see the day's work and what customers owe.
