Go to **Settings**, **Plan**, then **Manage plan**. Only the owner can change the plan.

## The plans

You pay by fleet size. Cars and motorbikes are counted separately (scooters, e-bikes and ATVs count as motorbikes; vans and pickups as cars).

| Plan | Fleet | Team accounts |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | Up to 2 cars and 5 motorbikes | 1 |
| **Starter** | Up to 5 cars and 10 motorbikes | 2 |
| **Pro** | Up to 15 cars and 25 motorbikes | Unlimited |
| **Business** | Any size; extra cars and motorbikes are charged each | Unlimited |

Everything that runs a rental is in every plan, even Free. Bigger plans add more ID scans, accounting export (from Starter), more than one location and your logo in the app (from Pro), and your booking page on your own web address (Business). Prices are on the page in your currency. Switch to **Yearly** to get a month free.

The plan that fits your fleet is marked **Fits your fleet**.

## Your free trial

You get 30 days with everything switched on. The page shows how many days are left.

## Choose and pay for a plan

1. Tap **Choose Pro** (or whichever plan).
2. **Pay to switch your plan on** appears at the top. Scan the PromptPay QR code with any Thai banking app, or transfer to the bank account shown.
3. Tap **Send the payment slip** and attach it.

We switch your plan on when the payment arrives, usually the same day. Changed your mind? Tap **Cancel this change**.

Moving to a smaller plan while paid up? You keep your current plan until the renewal date, then it changes. Nothing to pay.

## When the trial ends

You see **Your free trial has ended**. Your vehicles, customers and rentals are all still there. You can:

- **Choose a plan**
- **Continue on the Free plan**
- **Download my data**

If you picked Free during the trial, you just carry on with Free.

## If a payment is late

If your payment is more than 7 days overdue, or your plan is cancelled, you see **Your plan needs renewing**. Your data is safe. Tap **See plans** to renew.

Questions about paying? Tap **Write to us in Help** at the bottom of the page. See [Get help](/docs/get-help).

## Common questions

**What happens if I have more vehicles than my plan covers?**
Nothing is blocked. RoveHQ asks you to move up to a plan that fits within 14 days.

**Do I lose my data when the trial ends?**
No. Everything you saved is still there. Choose a plan or carry on with Free.
