Fully booked? Send the customer to another RoveHQ business that has a vehicle free, and they pay you 10% of the first rent as a one-time finder's fee. It works the other way too.

## Join

1. **Settings** > **Rentals and payments** > **Partner network**.
2. Switch on **Join the partner network**.
3. Under **Look for partners**, choose **In my town**, **In my province** or **Anywhere in the country**.

## Send a customer to a partner

1. Go to **Partners**.
2. Under **What does the customer want?**, set **From**, **To** (leave it blank for open-ended) and **Kind of vehicle**, then tap **Find**.
3. You see partners' free vehicles with their price and deposit. Tap **Choose** on one.
4. Enter the **Customer's name** and **Customer's phone**, then tap **Create the link**.
5. Tap **Copy link** or **Send on WhatsApp** to give it to the customer. The partner has already been told.

From then on the customer belongs to the partner. You only see how it's going (**Link sent**, **Booked**, **Rented** or **Canceled**) and your fee.

## When a partner sends you a customer

You rent to them as normal. Open it from **Customers partners sent you** with **Open booking**. When you record their first rent, the fee is set and is due within a week.

## Paying and getting paid

You settle the fee directly with each other, by PromptPay or bank transfer. If you owe one, it shows at the top of **Partners**. Pay it, then tap **Mark paid**.

That records a **Finder's fee paid** cost in your **Money in & out**, and a **Finder's fee received** in theirs. Fees you're owed show under **Customers you sent to partners** as paid or not paid yet.

## Common questions

**What if the customer never turns up?**
Then there's no fee. It's only set when the first rent is recorded.

**Who can see my vehicles?**
Only businesses that have also joined the partner network.
