Go to **Fleet** and tap **Import** (or **+**, then **Import vehicles**). Only the owner can import.

One file can hold any mix of vehicles, current rentals, customers, and payments and costs. Headers in any language, Buddhist years and any currency are fine.

Moving from other rental software? See [Switch from other rental software](/docs/switch-from-other-software).

## 1. Add your file

Use one of:

- **Choose a file**: Excel, CSV, a calendar (.ics) or a chat export (.txt).
- **Or a Google Sheets or calendar link**: share the sheet as "anyone with the link can view" first.
- **Photos**: up to 8 of blue books, notebook pages or handwritten lists. One page per photo, flat, good light.
- **Or paste messages from LINE or WhatsApp**.

Tap **Preview import**.

## 2. Check it

1. Answer any quick questions (like whether a rent is per day or per month).
2. Go through the **Vehicles**, **Rentals**, **Customers** and **Money** tabs. Fix anything wrong and untick **Bring in** on anything you don't want.
3. If a rental doesn't say which vehicle, pick one.

Cards marked **Needs a look** still come in and also land in your **To do**. If a column was read wrong, open **Change how a column was read**, fix it, and tap **Read it again**.

## 3. Bring it in

Tap **Bring it in**. Anything that couldn't be added is listed under **Not imported**.

## Undo an import

You have 30 days. Open the importer, find it under **Earlier imports**, and tap **Take this import back**. Everything it added is removed. Vehicles that were already there keep their filled-in details.

Got a notebook or something else? Tap **Notebook, photos or something else? Send it to us** and we'll help. See [Get help](/docs/get-help).

## Common questions

**Will it make copies of vehicles I already have?**
No. Vehicles already in your fleet are matched, and only their blank details are filled in.
