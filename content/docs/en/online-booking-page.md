Customers see your vehicles, prices and free dates and book at your listed price. You don't approve anything. The vehicle is held for them and the booking is confirmed when they sign.

## Turn it on

1. Open **Booking page** (in the menu under Tools).
2. Under **Page**, switch on **Let customers book online**.
3. Under **What customers can book**, choose monthly, set dates, or both.
4. Tap **Copy link** and share it (Facebook, LINE, Google listing). **Open page** shows it as a customer sees it.

Only vehicles with a price show up. Online bookings take your **Usual deposit** unless the vehicle has its own (set in **Settings** > **Rentals and payments**).

## Design and preview

Beside the editor you see your page as customers do. Switch between **Mobile** and **Desktop**. On a phone, tap **Preview**.

- **Design**: pick a template. The preview shows it straight away.
- **Sections**: switch sections on or off, move them up or down, and tap **Edit** to change what each one says.
- **Brand**: your logo and color.

Tap **Save changes** and the preview updates.

## Your address

Under **Address**:

- **Your address on Rove**: rove.rent/ plus a name you choose. Type it, tap **Save address**. Old links keep working.
- **Your own address on RoveHQ** (Pro plan and up): a short address that's easy to say on the phone.
- **Your own web address** (Business plan): e.g. book.yourshop.com. Tap **Connect** and add the one record RoveHQ shows you where you bought the domain, then **Check again**.

Have a website? Copy the code under **Booking box for your own website** and paste it in.

## Get found on rove.rent

In **Settings** > **Rentals and payments**, open **Listing on rove.rent** and switch on **List my vehicles on rove.rent**. People searching rove.rent by place and dates see your free vehicles and book on your page. No commission.

## When someone books

1. They pick a vehicle, dates and extras, type their name and phone, and tap **Book and continue**.
2. The vehicle is held for them (24 hours unless you change it in **Holds and notice**).
3. They add their details and ID photos, choose how to pay, and sign.

You get an alert ("New online booking...") and the booking appears in **Bookings**. If they say they've paid, tap **Confirm payment received** once the money is in.

If they don't sign in time, the dates open up again. In **Bookings** you can tap **Hold again**, or **Extend hold** while it's still held.

To stop same-day bookings or keep a cleaning day between rentals, see [Booking and price rules](/docs/booking-and-price-rules).

## Common questions

**Why isn't one of my vehicles on the page?**
It needs a daily, weekly or monthly price. Add one on the vehicle and it shows up.

**Does the customer pay when they book?**
No. On their booking link they choose to pay now (e.g. by bank transfer or a payment QR code, then send the slip) or when they get the vehicle. You confirm the payment when it arrives.
