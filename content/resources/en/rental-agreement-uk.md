UK hire has its own paperwork. Penalty charge notices for bus lanes, parking, the [London ULEZ and Congestion Charge](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/renting-a-car-advice-for-consumers/hiring-a-car-what-to-watch-out-for) and the Dartford Crossing all go to the registered keeper first. A hire firm can pass them to the hirer only with a copy of the hire agreement and a [statement of liability signed by the hirer](https://www.newham.gov.uk/parking-permits/penalty-charge-notices-hire-vehicles). This agreement includes that statement.

## What the UK version covers

**Licences.** GB licence holders give you a [DVLA check code](https://www.gov.uk/view-driving-licence), now valid for 21 days, so you can see categories and penalty points. Visitors may drive on their home licence for [up to 12 months](https://www.gov.uk/driving-nongb-licence).

**Fines.** Besides the PCN statement, the police can require you to name the driver under [section 172 of the Road Traffic Act 1988](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/52/section/172); the agreement says you will.

**Abroad.** A hire car leaving the UK needs your written permission and a VE103 vehicle-on-hire certificate.

**Fair terms.** Deposits and damage are limited to actual, evidenced loss, in line with [Part 2 of the Consumer Rights Act 2015](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/15/part/2). There is no 14-day cancellation right for hire on set dates ([regulation 28](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134/regulation/28)).

**Seats and limits.** Child seats until 12 years or 135 cm, and the [drink-drive limits](https://www.gov.uk/drink-drive-limit) for England, Wales, Northern Ireland and Scotland.

Every EU version also follows EU consumer law: prices shown with every compulsory charge included, damage charged only at its evidenced cost and never above the excess when a damage waiver applies, no keeping the whole deposit as a penalty, and the [14-day withdrawal right not applying to car rental for set dates](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2011/83/oj). Fines and renter details follow the GDPR, and the renter signs on paper or electronically, both valid under [eIDAS](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2014/910/oj).
