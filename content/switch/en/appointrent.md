Appointrent is Italian rental software for cars, motorbikes, scooters, bikes and vans. We couldn't find a public help article on exporting from it, so the steps above are general. Any Excel or CSV file works: RoveHQ reads the columns, shows you what it found, and you correct anything before it's saved. Dates written day first (05/06/2026 for 5 June) are fine.

## Free import

Importing is free on every plan. RoveHQ reads any Excel or CSV file. You see every vehicle, booking and payment before anything is saved, and you can take the whole import back within 30 days. If you'd rather not do it yourself, [send us the files](/contact) and we'll bring them in for you.
