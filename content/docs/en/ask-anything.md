Tap the round chat button at the bottom right of any page. On a phone it sits just above the menu bar. On a computer, **Ctrl+K** (**⌘+K** on a Mac) opens it too. It starts with suggestions for the page you're on, so you can often just tap one.

## Ask about your business

If you're the owner, it answers from your own records: vehicles, bookings, payments and costs, deposits, and tax, insurance and service dates. Nothing comes from outside RoveHQ. Try:

- "Who owes me money?"
- "What's due this week?"
- "Which of my vehicles is making the least money?"
- "When does its tax run out?" (on a vehicle's page, it knows which vehicle you mean)

Answers come with links to the vehicle, booking or report they're about. Ask a follow-up in the same chat if you want more.

## Tell it to record something

It can make four kinds of change:

- **Add a vehicle**: "Add a red Honda Click, plate 1กข 1234, 3,000 a month."
- **Record a payment or cost**: "I paid $450 for a new windscreen on the Ford Ecosport."
- **Create a booking link**: "Create a booking link for the Mazda 2 from 14 Oct, 11,000 a month, 5,000 deposit."
- **Mark paperwork as renewed**: "Renewed the Yaris's insurance until 3 May."

It shows a card with what it's about to save. Check it and tap **Confirm**, or **Cancel**. Wrong detail? Just say what to change. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

## Send a photo or file

Tap the paperclip (**Add files**) to send photos, PDFs, Word files or spreadsheets, up to 10 at a time. It works out what they are and gives you a button to the right place, with the file already there:

- A receipt goes to **Record money in or out**, filled in from the photo.
- Registration books, insurance policies and tax receipts go to the fleet assistant.
- Your own contract goes to **Rental agreement** in Settings, ready to read in.
- A spreadsheet goes to **Import**.
- Damage and odometer photos belong in the booking's handover or return check, so it opens the booking.

## Questions about using RoveHQ

Ask "How do I…" and it answers from these help guides, with a link to the right one. Staff get this part too. Still stuck? Tap **Talk to a person** and someone from RoveHQ replies in the same chat. See [Get help](/docs/get-help).

## Common questions

**Can it change something without me noticing?**
No. Every change is shown as a card first and nothing is saved until you press **Confirm**.

**Why can't my staff ask about money?**
Those answers read your money and profit, so they're for the owner only. Staff can still ask how to do things.
