Everything here is in **Settings**, **Business**, unless it says otherwise. Customers see these details on agreements, booking links and receipts.

## Business name and contact details

1. In **Your business**, fill in **Business name**, **Phone**, **Email** and **Address**.
2. Under **How customers reach you**, tap the apps you use (WhatsApp, LINE, Messenger and so on) and add your number or ID on each.
3. Tap **Save**.

## Agreement details

Tap **Agreement details** for **Legal name**, **Registration or tax number**, **Who signs for the business**, **Their job title** and a **Line at the bottom of agreements**. Tap **Save**.

To change the agreement terms, tap **Edit agreement terms**. See [Rental agreements](/docs/rental-agreements).

## Logo and colour

- **Upload logo**: PNG or JPG, wide works best. It saves as soon as you pick it.
- **Brand colour**: tap a colour or **Pick your own**, then **Save**.

Your logo and colour go on everything customers see. From the Pro plan your logo also replaces RoveHQ's inside the app, and you can untick **Show "Powered by RoveHQ"**.

## Your signature

Customers can't finish an online booking until this is done.

1. Save **Who signs for the business** and **Their job title** under **Agreement details** first.
2. In **Your signature**, tick the approval box.
3. Sign with your finger and tap **Save my signature**, or tap **Use a photo of my signature** (PNG or JPG, under 2 MB).

## Locations

In **Where you operate**, tap **Add a location**, enter a **Name** and **Address**, and tap **Add a location**. Use the pencil to edit, or the bin to remove (its vehicles stay, just without a location).

## Currency

Your currency and time zone are shown under your business details. The currency comes from the country you picked at setup. Your own app language is in **My account**.

## Plan and your data

**Settings**, **Plan** shows your plan, trial days left, and how many cars, motorbikes and team accounts you use. Tap **Manage plan** to change it (see [Plans and billing](/docs/plans-and-billing)). Tap **Download all records** to get everything in one file.

## Common questions

**Why can't customers finish their online booking?**
Your signature is missing. Add it in Settings, Business, under Your signature.
