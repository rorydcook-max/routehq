Open the vehicle, go to **Paperwork & service**, and open **Services and repairs**. You'll see the next service (by date, km or both, whichever comes first, with km left), the last service, the last oil change, and every job you've logged.

## Log a service or repair

1. Tap **Log a service or repair** (also under **More for this vehicle**).
2. Pick **What was done**: **Service**, **Oil change**, **Repair**, **Tires** or **Battery**.
3. Fill in **Date**, **Cost**, **Mileage** and **Garage or mechanic**.
4. Tick **Oil changed** if it was done during a service.
5. Set **Next due date**, **Next due at (km)**, or both.
6. Add a **Receipt photo** if you have one, and tap **Save**.

RoveHQ then:

- adds the cost to **Money in & out** (as Servicing, or Repair for repairs, tires and batteries)
- updates **Last service** (only for Service or Oil change) and **Last oil change**
- sets the next service, or clears an old due date you've now passed
- moves the mileage forward
- keeps the receipt in the vehicle's files

## Or start from the cost

Record a **Servicing**, **Maintenance** or **Repair** cost in **Money in & out** and pick the vehicle. It goes into the service history the same way. Use one or the other, not both.

## Set the next service only

**Edit** the vehicle, open **Tax, insurance and service dates**, set **Next service due** and **Oil change due**, then **Save changes**.

## Take it off the road

**More for this vehicle** > **Put in the shop**. The status becomes **In the shop** so nobody books it. When it's ready, tap **Back and free to rent**. Only works when the vehicle is free.

## Reminders

A late service shows on **Fleet** as **Service overdue**. Anything due within 7 days (or close on km) shows in **Today** on the dashboard. If the vehicle is out with a customer that week, they can get a message about it. See [Automatic customer messages](/docs/automatic-customer-messages).

## Common questions

**Do I need to add the cost in Money in & out as well?**
No. Logging a service with a cost records the cost for you. Adding it again counts it twice.
