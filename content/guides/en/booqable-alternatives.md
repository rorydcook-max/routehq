Booqable is a popular, well-made rental tool. Its homepage says it's used by "8,500+ business owners in 12+ industries", from party and AV hire to tools, boats and bikes. If you rent out vehicles, though, you may be looking for something built around cars and motorbikes rather than stock items.

This guide covers the main alternatives for small vehicle rental shops, what each costs and who it suits. Facts come from each company's own website **as of November 2026**, with links. Prices change, so check before you decide.

> We make RoveHQ, so we have an interest here. We've described every product from its own website and tried to be honest about where each one is the better choice, including Booqable itself.

## When Booqable is still the right choice

Booqable is strong if your business is mostly equipment, or a mix where vehicles are a small part. According to its [pricing page](https://booqable.com/pricing/), every plan includes unlimited inventory and orders with no commission, a ready-made booking page, website integration, and payments through Stripe, PayPal, Square, Adyen, Mollie and others. There's a mobile app for iOS and Android (an add-on at $9 to $12 per user a month), and a website builder add-on.

If you rent bikes, kayaks or e-scooters alongside other gear and want a polished online shop with a wide choice of payment providers, Booqable is a sensible pick. It's less suited if you need to record a vehicle's mileage and fuel, check a driver's licence, or produce a vehicle rental agreement.

## RoveHQ

RoveHQ is built for small fleets, from one vehicle to a few dozen cars, scooters, motorbikes, quads or vans, and it runs from your phone. You set it up yourself in a few minutes, with no sales call and no onboarding fee.

- **Price.** A [free plan](/pricing) for up to 2 cars and 5 motorbikes with no time limit. Starter is US$15, €13 or £11 a month (5 cars and 10 motorbikes), Pro $30, €27 or £22 (15 cars and 25 motorbikes) and Business from $45, €40 or £33, charged in your own currency. Yearly billing charges 11 months. Cars and motorbikes are counted separately, so a shop with 25 scooters fits Pro.
- **Payments.** Connect your own Stripe account and customers pay by card on your booking page, through embedded checkout or a payment link, or with Apple Pay and Google Pay. European methods such as SEPA Direct Debit, iDEAL, Bancontact, Klarna, MB Way and BLIK are available where Stripe offers them. Deposits can be held on the card, then released or captured. Cash, bank transfer and QR payments still work, and RoveHQ checks the slips customers send, including PromptPay and QRIS.
- **Platform fee.** Payments taken through Stripe carry a RoveHQ fee of 5% on Free, 2.5% on Starter, 2% on Pro, 1.5% on Business and 1% on any yearly plan, on top of Stripe's own processing fee. Booqable charges no commission, so if you take a lot by card, add this to the comparison.
- **Vehicle features.** Handover and return checks with odometer and fuel photos, a damage map and the customer's signature. Passport and licence photos are read automatically.
- **Agreements.** RoveHQ writes the rental agreement for each booking, in English and the local language in 18 countries across Southeast Asia and Europe, and the customer signs on their phone.
- **Messaging and languages.** Connect a LINE Official Account or Telegram bot and reply from one inbox; payment reminders go out automatically, and RoveHQ opens WhatsApp with the message ready to send. The app and customer pages come in 28 languages.
- **Where it falls short.** Stripe is the only card provider, there's no connection to travel booking sites, and no telematics integration.

## RentSyst

RentSyst describes itself on its [homepage](https://rentsyst.com/) as a "cloud CRM system for all types of car rental business", covering car rental, car sharing and taxi fleets, with GPS tracking and telematics built in. Its own [comparison with Booqable](https://blog.rentsyst.com/en/post/rentsyst-vs-booqable/) says it supports motorcycles, boats, RVs and bikes as well as cars.

The [pricing page](https://rentsyst.com/price/) lists Beginner at €55 a month for up to 15 vehicles, then per-vehicle pricing from €3 down to €2 as the fleet grows. Training, onboarding and setup packages are optional one-off extras (€50 to €300). There's a 14-day free version. RentSyst suits operators who want GPS and telematics connected to their bookings.

## HQ Rental Software

HQ says on its [FAQ](https://hqrentalsoftware.com/faqs) that it serves operators "from a small operator with 5 cars" to enterprises with 2,000 vehicles. It has a strong booking engine, a mobile app, many payment gateways, more than 30 languages and hosting in the USA, the Netherlands or Singapore.

Its [pricing page](https://hqrentalsoftware.com/pricing) lists Basic at $120 a month for 10 vehicles, Standard at $175 for 25 and Professional at $250 for 50, each with a one-time onboarding fee of $550 to $1,500. There's a 7-day free trial. HQ suits growing car and van businesses that sell through channels like Expedia.

## Rental Car Manager

Rental Car Manager's [homepage](https://www.rentalcarmanager.com/) says customers use it for "cars, campers, uber vehicle fleets, bikes/scooters, boats, Truck and Bus". It integrates with agents such as Rentalcars.com and Motorhome Republic, takes card payments through VostroPay, and offers online pre-check-in and electronic signing. Prices aren't on its site; third-party listings such as [Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/product/467170-Rental-Car-Manager/) show a starting price of A$90 a month, usage based. You can try a fully working trial system and keep your data if you stay.

## RENTALL (formerly Navotar) and TSD Rental

Both are aimed at bigger operations. [Auto Rental News reported in 2022](https://www.autorentalnews.com/news/navotar-announces-branding-switch-to-rentall) that Navotar had switched its products to the Rentall brand. RENTALL's [pricing page](https://www.rentallsoftware.com/pricing/) is quote-only and says its "minimum fleet size required to implement RENTALL is 10 vehicles", with some exceptions.

TSD's [car rental software page](https://tsdweb.com/car-rental-software/) says it's trusted by "10K+ car rental operators" in 75+ countries, and that the five largest rental companies in the world use its solutions. It also serves dealership loaner fleets. Pricing is by demo and quote. Both are capable systems, but they're not built for a five-scooter shop.

## Which one to choose

- **Mostly equipment, some bikes or scooters, want a polished online shop and your pick of payment providers:** stay with Booqable.
- **A small car, scooter, motorbike, quad or van shop you want to run from your phone, with card payments and deposits online, agreements written for you and a free plan to start:** try RoveHQ.
- **Need GPS and telematics tied to bookings:** look at RentSyst.
- **Growing car or van business, 10 to 50 vehicles, selling through booking sites:** look at HQ Rental Software.
- **Campervans, motorhomes or agent bookings:** look at Rental Car Manager.
- **Dealership loaners or 50+ vehicles across branches:** ask RENTALL or TSD for a demo.
