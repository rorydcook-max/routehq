Way2Rentals, from W4M Solutions, is rental software developed in Portugal for cars, vans, motorhomes and campervans in Portugal, Madeira, the Azores and Spain. We couldn't find a public help article on exporting from it, so the steps above are general. Any Excel or CSV file works: RoveHQ reads the columns, shows you what it found, and you correct anything before it's saved.

## Free import

Importing is free on every plan. RoveHQ reads any Excel or CSV file. You see every vehicle, booking and payment before anything is saved, and you can take the whole import back within 30 days. If you'd rather not do it yourself, [send us the files](/contact) and we'll bring them in for you.
