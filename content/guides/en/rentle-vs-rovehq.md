If you're searching for Rentle, you'll land on a different name. Rentle's [own news page](https://www.twicecommerce.com/blog/news/rentle-raises-3-8-million-euro-seed-round-to-power-circular-commerce) says "Rentle is now Twice Commerce", and rentle.io now redirects to twicecommerce.com. The product has grown into what its [homepage](https://www.twicecommerce.com/) calls a "circular commerce platform for rental and resale operators".

That's useful context for this comparison. TWICE is built for shops that rent and resell products (bikes, sports gear and the like), often across several stores. RoveHQ is built only for vehicle rental. They overlap for bike and e-bike rental, and not much beyond that. We've written this page anyway, because the honest answer is the useful one.

Facts about TWICE come from its own website **as of November 2026**.

## TWICE Commerce in brief

TWICE's [pricing page](https://www.twicecommerce.com/pricing) says every merchant gets every feature, and you pay by the number of inventory locations: €199 per location a month, with the first at 50% off. On the Standard tier there's no extra monthly fee, but TWICE takes a 1.5% commission on each payment it processes, plus usage-based costs for things like SMS. Enterprise starts at €5,000 a month with negotiated rates.

The feature list is long: serialised and pooled inventory, rental and resale pricing, subscriptions, a built-in online store with custom domains, multi-location stock, workflows, an API and more than 70 reports. Optional onboarding costs €999 (guided) or €1,990 (premium), and priority support €99 a month. There's a 14-day free trial with no card.

## Where TWICE is stronger

- **Multi-store retail.** One system for several shops and warehouses, with hours and fulfilment rules per location.
- **Rental plus resale.** Rent, sell, buy back and run subscriptions on the same items. Good for shops that sell ex-rental stock.
- **Online store.** A built-in storefront with a theme editor, checkout builder, custom domains and multiple languages.
- **Automation and API.** Workflows, webhooks, a bulk API and an MCP server for AI tools.

## Where RoveHQ is stronger

- **Vehicle rental features.** Handover and return checks with odometer and fuel photos, a damage diagram and signature; passport and licence photos read automatically; a rental agreement written for each booking in English and the local language.
- **Card deposits for vehicles.** Hold a deposit on the customer's card at pickup, then release it or capture what's owed at return.
- **Cost for a small shop.** A free plan and flat monthly prices by fleet size, from €13 a month. The platform fee on card payments is 1% on a yearly plan; on monthly plans it's 1.5% to 5%, so compare it with TWICE's 1.5% using your own card takings.
- **Every way customers pay.** Card, wallets and European methods online, and cash, transfer or QR with slips checked against what's owed (including PromptPay and QRIS).
- **Chat apps.** LINE and Telegram in one inbox with automatic rent reminders; WhatsApp opens with the message ready.

## Who should pick which

**Choose TWICE Commerce if** you run a bike, sports or similar shop, maybe with several locations, you rent and resell products, and you want a strong online store.

**Choose RoveHQ if** you rent cars, motorbikes, scooters, quads or vans from one or two bases, run things from your phone, and want card payments and deposits, vehicle handovers, licence checks and agreements handled for you.
