Money in is on the left, money out on the right. On a phone, tap **Money in** or **Money out** to switch sides.

![RoveHQ Money in & out: the month's money in, money out and profit, with each entry listed](/site/docs/screens/money-in-and-out.webp)

## Pick the period

Tap **Day**, **Week**, **Month**, **Year** or **Custom**. Use the arrows to go back or forward, and **Today** / **This month** etc. to jump back to now.

At the top you see **Money in**, **Money out** and **Profit** (or **Loss**) for that period. Refunds come off money in. Deposits aren't in these totals; they're in their own **Deposits** section at the bottom.

## Filter and search

- Tap a category chip, like **Fuel** or **Repair**, to show only that. Tap more to add them, or **All** to clear. The totals follow, so it's a quick way to see what you spent on fuel this month.
- The **Search** box finds the type, vehicle, customer, notes, who you paid or the booking number.

## Fix an entry

Tap it, change the **Amount**, **Date**, **Type**, **Vehicle** or **Notes** (for insurance or tax, also **Covers from** and **Covers until**), add or replace the receipt, and tap **Save changes**.

## Delete

- One entry: tap it, tap **Delete**. You get about five seconds to tap **Undo**.
- Several: tap **Select entries**, tick them, tap **Delete selected**, then **Yes, delete**. This one can't be undone.

## Spreadsheet for your accountant

Choose the period, then tap **Spreadsheet** (the download icon on a phone). You get every entry in that period.

## Yearly costs as monthly shares

In **Month**, **Year** and **Custom**, a payment that covers a long stretch, like a year of insurance, is split into monthly shares, so one month doesn't look terrible and the rest too good. Each share says something like "Share 3 of 12 · ฿12,000 paid 15 Mar". **Day** and **Week** show the real payment.

Opening a share edits or deletes the whole payment, not just that month.

## Common questions

**Why does my insurance show as a small amount each month?**
A cost that covers several months is split into a share per month. Switch to Day or Week to see the real payment on the day you paid.

**Why can't my staff see profit?**
Only the owner sees profit. Team members see money in and money out.
