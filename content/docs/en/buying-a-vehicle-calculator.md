Open **Buying a vehicle** from the menu.

## Check a vehicle

1. Pick the type: **Car**, **Scooter**, **Motorbike**, **Van**, **ATV** or **E-bike**.
2. Choose the **Make**, **Model** and **Year** (and **Version** if offered). Not on the list? Choose **Not listed** and type it.
3. Enter the **Price asked** and **Kilometres so far**.
4. Under **How you would rent it out**, pick **By the month**, **By the day** or **Both**.
5. Buying on finance? Tick **I would buy it on a loan** and enter the deposit, monthly payment and months.
6. Tap **Calculate earnings**.

A new model takes about a minute. RoveHQ looks up local shops' rental prices, used adverts for the model, and insurance, tax and servicing costs.

## Where the rent comes from

It uses the best it can find:

- if you rent by the month and already have this model, what you charge for it, adjusted for the age difference (if you change the rent, **Use that** puts it back)
- what at least 3 other rental businesses on RoveHQ near you (or in your country) charge for that model
- prices found online, which can be higher than what Facebook and walk-in shops charge

The note under the rent says which one was used. Change it if you know better.

## Reading the result

The top tells you roughly what it makes a year and gives a verdict: **Strong buy**, **Good buy**, **Only at a better price** or **Not worth it**. If the price is too high, it tells you the most to pay.

Then:

- **Is the price fair?**: where the price sits against similar adverts.
- **How it rents**: demand, the rent, and how much of the year it's likely out.
- **What it would make you**: cash left each month, profit a year, payback time and return.
- **What it costs to run**: insurance, tax and servicing a year.
- **Reliability and known issues**, and other vehicles worth a look for the same money.
- **When to sell**: the year it earns you the most, and what it should sell for.

A coloured line says whether figures were checked live today or are rough estimates. Open **The figures used** to change any number, and **Year by year** to see what you'd make selling after each year.

**Save to compare** keeps it in a list with your other checks. **Find better buys** searches current adverts around the same budget.

These are estimates. Check the vehicle and the numbers before you buy.

## Common questions

**The rent looks wrong for my area. Can I change it?**
Yes. Type your own price in the rent box under **How it rents**, or change any figure under **The figures used**. The result updates as you type.
