Only the owner can invite people.

## Invite someone

1. Go to **Settings**, **Team**.
2. Type their **Email**.
3. Under **What they can do**, pick a level (see below).
4. Choose **Their language** and tap **Send invite**.

They get an email, open the link, choose a password (8+ characters) and tap **Finish**. Until then they show as **Has not joined yet**.

## The levels

- **Teammate**: bookings, handovers, payments and customers. Refunds and waived charges go to the owner or a manager.
- **Manager**: the same, plus deciding refunds and waiving charges. No business settings.
- **Owner**: everything, including settings, billing and the team.

Managers and teammates don't see **Settings**, **Reports**, **Buying a vehicle** or the **Ask anything…** box, and can't import. On a vehicle page they don't see the purchase price, value or profit.

## Change or remove someone

In **Settings**, **Team**, under the person's name:

- **Level**: switch between **Manager** and **Teammate**. It saves straight away.
- **Send the invite again**: if they haven't joined.
- **Remove**, then **Yes, remove from the team**.

Your plan decides how many team accounts you get: Free 1, Starter 2, Pro and Business unlimited. See [Plans and billing](/docs/plans-and-billing).

## Common questions

**My teammate didn't get the email.**
Ask them to check spam, then tap Send the invite again under their name in Settings, Team.
