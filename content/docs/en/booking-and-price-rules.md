All of these are in **Settings** > **Rentals and payments**. They apply to bookings you make and ones customers make online. Only the owner can change them.

## Usual deposit

**Usual deposit**: type an amount and tap away. It's used for any vehicle without its own deposit. Leave it empty for no deposit.

## Discount for paying ahead

For monthly rentals. Open **Discount for paying ahead**, switch on **Offer a lower price for paying ahead**, then set **Months paid at once, at least** (e.g. 3), the **Lower price per month**, and **What the customer sees** (e.g. "Pay 3 months, save 10%"). Tap **Save**. Customers see it on their booking link.

## High-season prices

1. Open **High-season prices** > **Add a season**.
2. Give it a name, the **From** and **To** dates, and the **Change %** (e.g. 20). Use a minus number for a low-season discount.
3. Leave **Monthly prices too** off unless monthly renters should pay more as well.
4. Tap **Save**.

Only the days that fall in the season are charged more. You can still change the price on any booking.

## Paid extras

Open **Paid extras** > **Add an extra**. Name it, set the price, choose **Per day** or **Once per booking**, and whether it's for every vehicle, cars or bikes. Tap **Save**. Extras show on the booking form and your booking page, get added to the first payment, and go on the agreement.

## Travel area and limits

This goes into your agreements.

- **Home area**, country, and whether you're on an **Island** or the **Mainland**.
- **Whose law applies**.
- **Taking the vehicle to another island**: **Extra deposit** (set the amount), **Tell you first**, or **Not allowed**.
- **Vehicles are tracked by GPS**: adds a line telling the customer.
- **Fees and limits**: km per month, fuel per 1/8 tank, late fee, cleaning, smoking, emergency repair limit, days to return the deposit. Switch off anything you don't charge and it's left out of the agreement.

Tap **Save**.

## Holds and notice

Each one saves as soon as you change it.

- **Hold a vehicle for an unsigned booking for**: how long a new booking link keeps the vehicle before the dates open up again.
- **Notice needed for an online booking**: e.g. no bookings starting in the next 24 hours.
- **Keep free after each rental**: days for cleaning. Online bookings respect it; you can still book into it yourself.
- **Notice a customer gives before returning**: for open-ended rentals.
- **Notice needed to extend automatically**: extension requests made at least this far ahead are approved on their own if the vehicle is free. Later ones come to you.
