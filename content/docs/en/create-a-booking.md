Customer already has the vehicle? Use [Enter a rental that is already running](/docs/rentals-already-running) instead.

## Make the booking

1. **Bookings** > **New booking**. Keep **Create booking link** selected.
2. **Choose a vehicle**. If it's out, the start date jumps to the day it comes back.
3. On **Add customer details?** tap **Skip for now** (the customer fills it in) or **Add customer now** to pick a returning customer or add a new one.
4. Set the dates and price (below), tap **Continue**.
5. Choose the handover (below), tap **Continue**.
6. Check the summary and tap **Create booking link**.
7. Tap **Send to customer** or **Copy link**. If they're standing next to you, **Show a code to scan**.

## Dates and price

- Set **Rental start date** and **Rental end date**, or tick **Open-ended (no return date yet)** for a monthly rental with no end.
- **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly** or **Custom** is picked for you from the length. Change it if you want.
- The rate comes from the vehicle's price (plus any high-season price). Type over it if you agreed something else.
- **Deposit amount** comes from the vehicle, or your usual deposit.
- Under **What is included**, tick what comes with it (insurance, helmet, child seat). Tick any **Paid extras** the customer wants.
- **More options** lets you take several months (or weeks, days) upfront at a lower rate, change the currency, or add **Special conditions** to the agreement.

You'll see what the customer pays to start, e.g. "฿12,000 to start: first month's rent + deposit". Daily rentals are paid in one go; weekly and monthly start with the first period.

If the dates clash with another booking, RoveHQ tells you who has it.

## Handover

- **I deliver**: add the place and time, or leave them blank for now.
- **They collect**: leave the address blank to use your shop.
- **Decide later**: the customer can suggest a place and time on their link.

## Starting today?

You'll see **Starting today**. Either **Share booking link with customer**, or tap **Payment already collected? Record it now**, enter the amount, deposit and method, and tap **Record walk-in rental**. The rental starts straight away.

## After you send it

The vehicle is held for the customer. They open the link, add their details and passport and licence photos, choose how to pay, and sign. Once they sign, the booking is confirmed and the payment schedule is set up. You can follow each step on the booking.

## The booking page

Tap any booking in **Bookings** to open it. At the top: **Start handover**, **Start return**, **Change terms**, and **More** (**Edit booking**, **Change vehicle**, **Add a cost**, **Cancel booking**). Below are the tabs: **Handover & contact**, **Payments**, **Vehicle & deposit**, **Customer**, **Agreement** and **Messages**.

## Common questions

**Do I need the customer's details first?**
No. Tap **Skip for now** and the customer fills in their own details, ID photos and signature on the link.

**How long does the link work?**
7 days. **Resend booking link** on the booking gives it another 7. The vehicle is only held for the time set in **Holds and notice** (24 hours unless you change it).
