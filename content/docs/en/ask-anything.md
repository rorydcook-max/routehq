The **Ask anything…** box is at the top of the menu (on a phone, the magnifying glass at the top of the screen). Press **Ctrl+K** (**⌘+K** on a Mac) to open it from any page. Only the owner has it.

## Ask about your business

It answers from your own records: vehicles, bookings, payments and costs, deposits, and tax, insurance and service dates. Nothing comes from outside RoveHQ. Try:

- "Which of my vehicles is making the least money?"
- "What income can I expect over the next 6 months?"
- "Which tax, insurance or service is due next?"
- "Who owes me money right now?"

Answers come with links to the vehicle, booking or report they're about. Ask a follow-up in the same chat if you want more.

## Tell it to record something

It can make four kinds of change:

- **Add a vehicle**: "Add a red Honda Click, plate 1กข 1234, 3,000 a month."
- **Record a payment or cost**: "I paid 5,000 baht for a new windscreen on the Ford Ecosport."
- **Create a booking link**: "Create a booking link for the Mazda 2 from 14 Oct, 11,000 a month, 5,000 deposit."
- **Mark paperwork as renewed**: "Renewed the Yaris's insurance until 3 May."

It matches the vehicle or customer by name, plate or colour, then shows a card with what it's about to save. Check it and tap **Confirm**, or **Cancel**. Wrong detail? Just say what to change and it redoes the card. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

## Questions about using RoveHQ

Ask "How do I…" and it answers from these help guides, with a link to the right one. Staff get this part too. If that doesn't sort it, tap **Send this to the RoveHQ team** and the conversation goes to us in **Help**, where you'll see our reply. See [Get help](/docs/get-help).

## Common questions

**Can it change something without me noticing?**
No. Every change is shown as a card first and nothing is saved until you press **Confirm**.

**Why don't my staff have it?**
It reads your money and profit, so it's for the owner only.
