## Add a loan

Only the owner can do this.

1. Open the vehicle and tap **Edit**.
2. Open **Loan**.
3. Fill in **Sale price**, **Down payment**, **Monthly payment**, **Loan length (months)** and **Months left**. For a new loan, leave **Months left** empty and it uses the full length.
4. Tap **Save changes**.

As you type, RoveHQ shows what you **Borrowed**, the **Total you pay**, the **Interest** (as a flat rate a year, the way Thai hire purchase is quoted) and what's **Still to pay**. The monthly payment and months left are enough to see what's still to pay.

You can fill this in when you add a vehicle too.

## See it on the vehicle

**Paperwork & service** > **Loan** shows the monthly payment, still to pay, months left, end date, total, interest and **Paid so far**. The end date also appears as **Loan ends** in the tax and insurance dates.

**Months left** counts down by itself each month. If you pay some off early, change it in **Edit**. To remove the loan, empty all its boxes there and tap **Save changes**.

## Record payments

Each time you pay, add a cost in **Money in & out** with type **Finance** and pick the vehicle. See [Add a cost](/docs/add-a-cost).

## Common questions

**Does RoveHQ record my loan payments?**
No. Record each one in **Money in & out** as a **Finance** cost for the vehicle. **Paid so far** adds them up.
