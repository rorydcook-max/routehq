Open **Calendar**. There are two views at the top.

## Agenda

What's happening each day:

- handovers and returns, including ones that are late,
- payments due, and unpaid ones,
- road tax, insurance, compulsory insurance and services due on your vehicles,
- jobs from your To do list.

Colors: blue is a handover, amber a return, purple a payment, red is late or overdue, green is done.

Switch between **Day**, **Week**, **Month** and **Custom** (pick your own **From** and **To** dates). Use **Today** to jump back. Tap anything to open it.

## By vehicle

One row per vehicle across the month, so you can see at a glance which are **On rent**, **Booked**, **Late** or free. The top shows how many are free today.

- Tap a booking to open it.
- Tap an empty day to start a new booking for that vehicle from that day.

## In your own calendar app

Want handovers and returns in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook? Go to **Settings** > **Integrations** > **Calendar feed** and tap **Create my calendar address**. It updates by itself. Keep the address private: anyone with it can see your bookings.
