# LINE and WhatsApp message templates for rental businesses

Copy a message, replace everything in {braces}, check the numbers and send. Keep each one as a quick reply so you never type it twice.

## Placeholders used

| Placeholder | Example |
|---|---|
| {renter_name} | Sarah |
| {business_name} | Island Wheels |
| {staff_name} | Nok |
| {vehicle} | Honda Click 160, white |
| {plate} | 1กข 1234 |
| {start_date} / {end_date} | 3 Nov / 10 Nov |
| {pickup_time} / {return_time} | 10:00 / 10:00 |
| {price_per_day} / {total_price} | ฿250 / ฿1,750 |
| {deposit_amount} | ฿3,000 |
| {payment_details} | Bank, account name and number, or PromptPay / QR |
| {location} | Shop address or Google Maps link |
| {phone} | Your emergency number |
| {booking_link} | Your online booking page |

> Tip: send the payment details as text, not only as a photo of a QR code. Renters can copy text into their banking app without mistakes.

---

## 1. Booking enquiry: reply with price

Hi {renter_name}, thanks for your message! Yes, we have a {vehicle} available from {start_date} to {end_date}.

Price: {price_per_day} per day, {total_price} in total.
Deposit: {deposit_amount}, refunded when the vehicle comes back in the same condition.
Included: {included_items, e.g. 2 helmets, phone holder, basic insurance}.

Would you like me to reserve it for you? I just need your full name, a photo of your passport or ID and your driving licence.

## 2. Booking confirmation

Your booking is confirmed, {renter_name}.

Vehicle: {vehicle}
Pickup: {start_date} at {pickup_time}, {location}
Return: {end_date} at {return_time}
Total: {total_price}, deposit {deposit_amount}

Please bring your passport or ID and your driving licence. See you on {start_date}!

## 3. Payment or deposit request

Hi {renter_name}, to secure your booking for {start_date} to {end_date}, please transfer {amount_due} ({what_it_covers, e.g. deposit / first week / full payment}).

{payment_details}

Once paid, please send a photo of the slip here. We'll hold the vehicle until {hold_until_time}.

## 4. Deposit or payment received

Thank you, {renter_name}. We've received {amount_received} on {payment_date} for booking {booking_number}.

Paid so far: {total_paid}
Remaining: {balance_due}, due {balance_due_date}

Your deposit of {deposit_amount} will be refunded after the vehicle is returned and checked.

---

## 5. Pickup details: day before

Hi {renter_name}, a quick reminder about tomorrow.

Pickup: {start_date} at {pickup_time}
Where: {location}
Please bring: passport or ID, driving licence, and {remaining_payment} if not yet paid.

The handover takes about 15 minutes: we walk round the vehicle together, take photos and sign the checklist. See you tomorrow!

## 6. Delivery details: day before

Hi {renter_name}, we're delivering your {vehicle} tomorrow, {start_date}, at around {delivery_time}.

Delivery address: {delivery_address}
Our staff member {staff_name} will call you on arrival.

Please have your passport or ID and driving licence ready. If your plans change, just message us here.

## 7. Welcome message at handover

Welcome, {renter_name}! Your {vehicle}, plate {plate}, is ready. A few things to keep in mind:

- Helmets: always wear one, driver and passenger. It's the law and police do check.
- Fuel: you received it at {fuel_level_out}. Please return it at the same level, or we charge {fuel_charge}.
- Return: {end_date} at {return_time}, at {return_location}. Late returns are charged {late_fee}.
- Parking: please lock the steering and keep the key with you.
- Emergency or breakdown: call or message {phone}, any time.

Your handover photos and checklist are saved. Enjoy your ride!

## 8. Mid-rental check-in (long rentals)

Hi {renter_name}, just checking in. How's the {vehicle} running?

If you notice anything (a warning light, a soft tyre, strange noises), please tell us early and we'll sort it. The next service is due at {service_km} km; we'll arrange it with you when it's close. Thanks for looking after it!

---

## 9. Return reminder: day before

Hi {renter_name}, a friendly reminder that your {vehicle} is due back tomorrow, {end_date}, at {return_time}, at {return_location}.

Please return it with fuel at {fuel_level_out}. If you'd like to keep it longer, reply here today and we'll check availability.

## 10. Return reminder: morning of

Good morning, {renter_name}! See you today at {return_time} at {return_location} for the return of your {vehicle}.

The check takes about 10 minutes. Your deposit refund of {deposit_amount} will be sent after the check, to {refund_method}.

## 11. Late return: polite

Hi {renter_name}, your {vehicle} was due back at {return_time} today. Is everything OK?

Please let us know when you can return it. If you need more time, we can extend at {price_per_day} per day.

## 12. Late return: firm

Hi {renter_name}, the {vehicle} (plate {plate}) is now {hours_late} hours overdue and we haven't heard from you.

As set out in your rental agreement, late returns are charged {late_fee}, and this will be deducted from your deposit. Please return the vehicle by {final_deadline} or call us on {phone}.

If we cannot reach you by then, we will follow the steps in the agreement for an unreturned vehicle, which may include reporting it to the police.

## 13. Extension offer

Hi {renter_name}, your rental ends on {end_date}. Would you like to keep the {vehicle} longer?

We can extend it at {extension_price} ({e.g. per day / for another week / monthly rate}). Just reply with your new return date and we'll confirm and send the payment details.

---

## 14. Monthly rent due

Hi {renter_name}, your monthly rent for the {vehicle} is due on {due_date}.

Amount: {monthly_amount} for {period_start} to {period_end}
{payment_details}

Please send the slip here once paid. Thank you for renting with us again this month!

## 15. Damage found at return

Hi {renter_name}, thanks for returning the {vehicle}. During the return check we found {damage_description} that wasn't on the handover checklist. Photos from handover and return are attached.

The repair cost is {repair_cost}, based on {quote_source, e.g. a quote from our garage / the price list in your agreement}. As set out in the agreement, we'll deduct this from your deposit and refund the remaining {refund_amount}.

If you'd like to discuss it, reply here or call {phone}. We're happy to show you the vehicle and the photos.

## 16. Deposit refund sent

Hi {renter_name}, your deposit refund has been sent.

Deposit: {deposit_amount}
Deductions: {deductions, or "none"}
Refunded: {refund_amount} on {refund_date} to {refund_method}

Thanks for looking after the {vehicle}. It was a pleasure!

## 17. Review request

Thanks again for renting with {business_name}, {renter_name}. If you have a minute, a short review helps a small business like ours a lot:

{review_link}

And if anything wasn't right, please tell us here so we can fix it.

---

## 18. Fine received after the rental

Hi {renter_name}, we've received a traffic fine for the {vehicle} (plate {plate}) dated {offence_date} at {offence_time}, during your rental.

Fine: {fine_type, e.g. speeding / red light}, amount {fine_amount}. A copy is attached.

As set out in your rental agreement, fines during the rental are the renter's responsibility. You can {how_to_pay, e.g. pay it online with the reference below / transfer the amount to us and we'll pay it and send you the receipt}.

Reference: {fine_reference}

Please let us know by {reply_by_date}. Thank you.

## 19. Cancellation reply: refund due

Hi {renter_name}, no problem, your booking for {start_date} to {end_date} is cancelled.

Under our cancellation policy, you'll receive {refund_amount}. We'll send it to {refund_method} within {refund_days} days and message you when it's done.

We hope to see you another time!

## 20. Cancellation reply: outside the free cancellation period

Hi {renter_name}, we're sorry your plans have changed. Your booking for {start_date} is cancelled.

As the cancellation was made {notice_given, e.g. less than 48 hours} before pickup, our policy allows a refund of {refund_amount} out of the {amount_paid} paid. If you'd prefer, we can move your booking to new dates within {rebook_window} instead, at no extra charge.

Just reply with what suits you best.
