FareHarbor reports let you choose dates, filters, groupings and columns. RoveHQ recognises the bookings export by its contact name, email and phone columns and reads each booking as a rental. FareHarbor is US-based, so dates that could be read either way are read month first, and RoveHQ asks you to confirm.

## Free import

Importing is free on every plan. RoveHQ recognises FareHarbor exports and reads them straight away. You see every vehicle, booking and payment before anything is saved, and you can take the whole import back within 30 days. If you'd rather not do it yourself, [send us the files](/contact) and we'll bring them in for you.
