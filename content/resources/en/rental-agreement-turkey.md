Türkiye changed the rules for rental businesses twice in 2026. Since [Law No. 7574](https://orgtr.org/7574-karayollari-trafik-kanununda-degisiklik-yapilmasina-dair-kanun/) (February), a traffic fine on a rented vehicle is issued to the renter when the police's rental records (KABİS) show who had it that day. And from 1 January 2027 the Ministry of Trade's [rental regulation](https://www.hukukihaber.net/motorlu-kara-tasitlarinin-kiralanmasi-hakkinda-yonetmelik/amp) sets how deposits, damage, cancellations and late returns work for car and van rentals. This agreement is written for both.

## What the Türkiye version covers

**KABİS and fines.** The renter's identity (T.C. Kimlik No, or passport and nationality) and license are recorded so you can enter the rental on KABİS, and fines go to the renter, not you.

**Deposits.** No more than three days' rent for rentals up to six days and seven days' rent up to 29 days, refunded within 7 days, never a check or senet.

**Damage.** Charged only with an independent expert's report and the handover and return documents; no loss-of-value charge without an arbitration or court decision.

**Licenses.** Class B for cars, M for mopeds, A1/A2/A for motorbikes and B1 for quads. A car license alone doesn't cover a 125 cc motorbike in Türkiye, and tourists can drive on their own license for six months from entry ([Highway Traffic Regulation art. 88](https://www.alomaliye.com/2020/06/05/karayollari-trafik-yonetmeliginde-degisiklik-05-06-2020/)).

**Consumer law and data.** Consumer Protection Law No. 6502 and the unfair-terms regulation, no 14-day withdrawal for set dates, the renter's KVKK rights, and no sharing of renters on blacklists ([KVKK decision 2020/335](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/6909/2020-335)).
