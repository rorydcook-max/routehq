On a computer the menu is down the left. On a phone the bottom bar has **Home**, **To do**, **Inbox** and the **+** button; everything else is under **More**. **My account**, language and **Sign out** are at the bottom of the menu (on a phone, under **More**).

## The menu

- **Dashboard** (**Home** on a phone): today's handovers, returns, money due and messages. See [Dashboard](/docs/dashboard).
- **To do**: payments to collect and jobs for the team, soonest first. See [To do](/docs/to-do).
- **Inbox**: customer chats from your connected apps in one place. See [Inbox and messaging](/docs/inbox-and-messaging).
- **Bookings**: every rental, from booking link to return. See [Create a booking](/docs/create-a-booking).
- **Calendar**: what's happening each day, and which vehicles are out or free. See [Calendar](/docs/calendar).
- **Fleet**: your vehicles, prices and paperwork. See [Add a vehicle](/docs/add-a-vehicle).
- **Customers**: their documents, what they have now and what they've spent. See [Customers](/docs/customers).
- **Money in & out**: every payment and every cost. See [Money in and out](/docs/money-in-and-out).
- **Reports**: how the business is doing. See [Reports](/docs/reports).
- **Documents**: signed agreements, customer ID and photos. See [Documents](/docs/documents).
- **Partners**: fully booked? Send the customer to a partner and earn a share of the first rent. See [Partners](/docs/partners).
- **Buying a vehicle**: what a vehicle would earn before you buy it. See [Buying a vehicle calculator](/docs/buying-a-vehicle-calculator).
- **Settings**: business details, payments, messaging, alerts, team and plan. See [Business settings](/docs/business-settings).
- **Help**: guides, and a place to write to us. See [Get help](/docs/get-help).

A number next to **To do**, **Inbox** or **Help** means something is waiting.

If you run more than one business, pick which one at the top of the menu.

## The + button

Tap **+** (on a computer it's the round **Quick add** button at the top of the menu) for:

- **New booking**, **Hand over a vehicle**, **Take a vehicle back**, **Record money in or out**, **Upload a receipt**
- Under **Less often**: **Add a customer**, **Add a vehicle**, **Tell the assistant**, **Import vehicles**

## Ask a question

The owner gets an **Ask anything…** box at the top of the menu (on a phone, the magnifying glass at the top of the screen). Ask about your own records ("Who owes me money right now?") or tell it to record a cost. Nothing changes until you press **Confirm**.

## What to set up first

1. Finish the two setup steps. See [The setup checklist](/docs/setup-checklist).
2. Add your vehicles: [one by one](/docs/add-a-vehicle), [with the assistant](/docs/fleet-assistant) or [from a spreadsheet](/docs/import-a-spreadsheet).
3. Add who's renting now. See [Rentals already running](/docs/rentals-already-running).
4. Connect a chat app so reminders go out by themselves. See [Automatic customer messages](/docs/automatic-customer-messages).
5. Add your signature and how customers pay you. See [Business settings](/docs/business-settings) and [Payment reminders and methods](/docs/payment-reminders-and-methods).
6. Share your [booking page](/docs/online-booking-page).
7. Turn on [alerts](/docs/notifications) and [invite your team](/docs/invite-your-team).

The checklist on your **Dashboard** follows this order and ticks things off as you go.

## Common questions

**Why can't my staff see Settings or Reports?**
Settings, Reports and Buying a vehicle are owner-only. Managers and teammates don't see them in the menu.
