RoveHQ writes the agreement for every booking, in English and the language of your business location (Thai in Thailand). You don't write one, you just add your signature and any extra terms.

**Settings** > **Business** > **Rental agreement** shows the agreement itself, filled in with a sample booking. Tap **Open full size** to read it in a new tab.

Already have your own contract? Tap **Upload your contract** beside it. See [Use your own agreement](/docs/use-your-own-agreement).

## Add your signature

Customers can't finish a booking until you do this.

If RoveHQ shows **Add your signature first** when you make a booking, tap **Add my signature**, then:

1. Type **Your full name** and **Your role** (e.g. Owner).
2. Sign in the box with your finger, or tap **Use a photo of my signature** (JPG or PNG under 2 MB).
3. Tick the box and tap **Save my signature**.

Or do it in **Settings** > **Business** > **Your signature**: tick the box, then sign with your finger and tap **Save my signature**, or tap **Use a photo of my signature**. Fill in **Who signs for the business** and **Their job title** under **Agreement details** first.

## Check your business details

**Settings** > **Business** > **Agreement details**: legal name, tax number, who signs and the line at the bottom. Tap **Save**. Your logo and brand colour go on the agreement too.

## Add your own terms

1. **Settings** > **Business** > **Rental agreement** > **Edit agreement terms**.
2. Below the agreement, under **Your own terms**, write each rule as a plain sentence, e.g. "No pets in the vehicle." Tap **Add another term** for more. The agreement above shows them as you type.
3. Tap **Save**.

They're added at the end of every new agreement and numbered for you.

Fees and limits in the agreement (late fee, fuel charge, cleaning, how far the vehicle can go) are listed under **Your numbers in the agreement**. Tap **Change these numbers** to edit them. See [Booking and price rules](/docs/booking-and-price-rules).

## How the customer signs

On their booking link the customer adds their details and passport and licence photos, taps **Save details and go to signing**, reads the final agreement, ticks the box and signs with their finger. They must sign with the name on the booking.

To find it later, open the booking and go to the **Agreement** tab. You can view or download each document there, plus a **Signing certificate** showing who signed and when. The customer sees it on their own booking page too.

## Get a change signed

If you change the rate or deposit (**Change terms** > **Change rate or deposit**), extend with **Customer signs the change (recommended)** ticked, or swap the vehicle after they've signed, the customer gets a short change form to sign. Nothing changes until they sign.

The booking shows **Waiting for the customer to sign**. Tap **Send to customer** or **Copy link**. **See what they will see** shows you their screen. Made a mistake? **Cancel this change**.

## Common questions

**If I change my terms, do signed agreements change too?**
No. New terms only go into agreements that haven't been signed yet.
