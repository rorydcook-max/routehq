Go to **Settings**, then **Plan**. Only the owner can change the plan.

## The plans

You pay by fleet size. Cars and motorbikes are counted separately (scooters, e-bikes and ATVs count as motorbikes; vans and pickups as cars).

| Plan | Fleet | Team accounts |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | Up to 2 cars and 5 motorbikes | 1 |
| **Starter** | Up to 5 cars and 10 motorbikes | 2 |
| **Pro** | Up to 15 cars and 25 motorbikes | Unlimited |
| **Business** | Any size; extra cars and motorbikes are charged each | Unlimited |

Everything that runs a rental is in every plan, even Free. Bigger plans add more ID scans, accounting export (from Starter), more than one location and your logo in the app (from Pro), and your booking page on your own web address (Business). Prices are shown in your currency. Switch to **Yearly** to get a month free.

The plan that fits your fleet is marked **Fits your fleet**.

## Your free trial

You get 30 days with everything switched on. The page shows how many days are left.

## Subscribe

1. Choose **Monthly** or **Yearly**, then tap **Choose Pro** (or whichever plan).
2. The **Checkout** opens inside RoveHQ. Pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, or a local method Stripe offers where you are. Payments are handled by Stripe; RoveHQ never sees your card number.
3. Your plan switches on as soon as the payment goes through.

## Your plan

The **Plan** tab shows:

- **Price** and when you pay next
- **Payment method**, the card on file
- **Billing address** and **Tax ID**, for your invoices
- **Invoices**, each one to **View** or download as a **PDF**

## Change plan

Tap **Change plan** and pick another one.

- **Moving up** happens straight away. You pay the difference for the rest of the current period.
- **Moving down** happens at the end of the period you've paid for. If the smaller plan covers fewer vehicles, you choose which ones to keep; the rest are **Parked**: still in RoveHQ with all their history, but they can't take new bookings until your plan covers them again.

## Cancel

Tap **Cancel plan**. You keep your plan until the end of the period you've paid for, then move to **Free**. Nothing is deleted.

## Update payment method

Tap **Update payment method** to add a new card. If a payment fails, you see **Payment failed — update card**. Tap it and add a card that works. Your data stays safe meanwhile.

## When the trial ends

You see **Your free trial has ended**. Your vehicles, customers and rentals are all still there. You can:

- **Choose a plan**
- **Continue on the Free plan**
- **Download my data**

If you picked Free during the trial, you just carry on with Free.

Questions about paying? Tap **Write to us in Help** at the bottom of the page. See [Get help](/docs/get-help).

## Common questions

**What happens if I have more vehicles than my plan covers?**
Nothing is blocked. RoveHQ asks you to move up to a plan that fits within 14 days.

**Do I lose my data when the trial ends?**
No. Everything you saved is still there. Choose a plan or carry on with Free.

**How do I pay?**
By card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, or a local method Stripe offers in your country, at a secure checkout inside RoveHQ. Plans are priced in your currency.
