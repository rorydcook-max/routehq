Go to **Fleet**, tap **Import**, and answer **Where is your data now?** Only the owner can import.

## 1. Pick your software

Tap **Rental software** and choose yours from the list. If it isn't there, tap **Other software**.

RoveHQ shows the steps for getting your data out of that system, with a link to its own help article where there is one.

## 2. Export from your old software

Most systems export from the bottom or top of each list (bookings or orders, customers, vehicles or inventory) or from **Reports**. Download each as **CSV** or **Excel**. If you can't find it, ask their support for an export of your data.

Export everything you can: vehicles, customers, bookings, and payments if they're separate.

## 3. Upload the files together

Upload up to 8 files at once. RoveHQ links them for you: bookings go to the right vehicle by plate and to the right customer by name, phone or ID.

For recognised systems you'll see **read as a … export** next to each file. Those are read straight away, with no guessing.

## 4. Check and bring it in

Go through the **Vehicles**, **Rentals**, **Customers** and **Money** tabs, untick anything you don't want, and tap **Bring it in**. See [Import a spreadsheet](/docs/import-a-spreadsheet) for the details of this step.

How things come across:

- Upcoming and current bookings become bookings. Finished ones come in as returned.
- Cancelled bookings, quotes and drafts are listed but left unticked. Tick any you want.
- Security deposits are recorded as deposits held, not income.
- Dates and miles or kilometres are read the way your old software writes them.

Changed your mind? You have 30 days to **Take this import back** under **Earlier imports**.

## Stuck?

Send the files in [Help](/docs/get-help) and we'll bring them in for free.
