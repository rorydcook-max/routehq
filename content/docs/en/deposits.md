## Set the amount

Each booking takes the vehicle's own deposit, or your **Usual deposit** from Settings if the vehicle has none. You can change it on the booking form. See [Booking and price rules](/docs/booking-and-price-rules).

## Take the deposit

The deposit is part of the first payment. You record it one of these ways:

- **Cash at handover**: on the last step of the handover check, enter the **Deposit amount received** and tap **Confirm cash and make receipt**.
- **Paid another way**: on the booking's **Payments** tab, find the deposit and tap **Record payment**.
- **Rental already running**: tick **You are holding the ... deposit** when you enter it.

The booking then shows the deposit as held.

## Settle it at return

The return check ends with a **Deposit** step:

1. RoveHQ shows the deposit you hold and anything still owed on the booking.
2. Add what you're taking off: **Fuel missing**, **Damage**, **Cleaning fee**.
3. RoveHQ shows what to **Give back to the customer**. Tap **Give back a different amount** if you agreed something else.
4. Choose how you're giving it back: **Cash**, **Bank transfer** or **PromptPay**.

If the deductions are more than the deposit, tick **Bill the customer the ... the deposit does not cover** and it's added to the booking for them to pay.

Don't know the repair cost yet? Tick **Damage cost not known yet: keep the deposit for now**. Nothing is given back, and pricing the damage goes on your To do list. Add the cost when you know it ([Charge a renter](/docs/charge-a-renter)), then return what's left.

## Return or keep it later

On the booking, open the **Vehicle & deposit** tab and find **Refunds & deposit**. It shows what you took, returned, kept, and still hold.

- **Return deposit**: enter the amount and tap **Confirm return**.
- **Record deduction**: enter the amount and a reason (damage, late return, unpaid rent) and tap **Confirm deduction**. Keeping the whole deposit is the same thing, for the full amount, when the costs really add up to it. The RoveHQ agreement lets you deduct only what the renter actually owes, never the whole deposit as a penalty.

## How it shows in Money in & out

Deposits are held for customers, so they're listed separately under **Deposits** and don't count as income or cost. When you keep part or all of it, that part counts as income.

## Common questions

**Why doesn't the deposit show in my income?**
Because it's the customer's money until you keep it. Deposits are listed separately in **Money in & out**. Anything you keep or deduct does count as income.
