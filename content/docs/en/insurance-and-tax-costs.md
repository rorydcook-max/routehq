Paid ฿12,000 for a year of insurance? If it all landed in one month, that month would look awful. So RoveHQ counts 1/12 of it each month it covers.

You pay one of two ways:

- **All at once**: one cost on the day you paid, shared across the months it covers.
- **Every month**: RoveHQ adds each instalment as a cost on the same day every month until the cover runs out. You don't enter them.

## Renew from the vehicle (best way)

This records the cost and moves the car's date in one go.

1. Open the vehicle, find **Tax, insurance and service dates**.
2. Under the one you renewed, tap **Renewed it? Enter the new date** (or **Add the date**).
3. Add a **Photo of the document (optional)**. RoveHQ reads the date and cost from it.
4. Check **Cover starts** and **Runs out on**.
5. Under **How is it paid?** pick **All at once** or **Every month**, and enter the **Cost** (or **Cost per month**).
6. Tap **Save**.

It tells you what it'll do, like "Counted as ฿1,000 a month over 12 months in your costs".

**Thailand, older vehicles:** cars over 7 years and bikes over 5 need an inspection (ตรอ.) before road tax. When you renew **Road tax**, put that in **Inspection cost (ตรอ.)**. It's saved as its own **Other** cost over the same months. Leave it empty if there wasn't one.

## Or add it as a normal cost

1. Add a cost (receipt photo or by hand) and choose **Insurance** or **Tax**. A **What it covers** box appears.
2. If there's more than one paper it could be, pick it under **Which of the car's papers?** (or **Not the car's papers**).
3. Fill in **Covers from** and **Covers until**.
4. Leave **Also set the car's ... to run out on ...** ticked to move the car's date too.
5. **Save**.

You can change the cover dates later by opening the entry in **Money in & out**.

## Names by country

Thailand: **Road tax**, **Compulsory insurance (Por Ror Bor)**, **Insurance**. Elsewhere RoveHQ adds the local name, e.g. Malaysia **Road tax (LKM)** and **Vehicle inspection (PUSPAKOM)**, Indonesia **(PKB / STNK)** and **(KIR)**, Philippines **(LTO)**, **(CTPL)** and **(Emission test)**, Vietnam **(Phí đường bộ)**, **(Bảo hiểm TNDS)** and **(Đăng kiểm)**.

## Common questions

**How are the months counted?**
The way you pay: 15 March to 14 March next year is 12 months, so a ฿12,000 policy counts ฿1,000 a month.
