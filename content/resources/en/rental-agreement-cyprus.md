The question every Cyprus renter asks is whether they can drive to the north. Insurance from the Republic [is not valid there](https://www.cyprus-faq.com/en/south/transport/north-cyprus-border-crossing-insurance), and [many local hire firms won't allow it](https://www.rac.co.uk/drive/travel/country/cyprus). This agreement makes it your choice: no crossing without your written permission and separate insurance bought by the renter, who is then fully responsible there.

## What the Cyprus version covers

**Language.** English beside Greek. Instead of making one version prevail, the meaning more favourable to the renter applies, as EU unfair-terms law reads doubtful terms anyway.

**Driving.** Left-hand traffic, child restraints under 12 years and 150 cm, and the alcohol limits for new and professional drivers.

**Fines.** Camera fines are transferred to the renter through the police.

**Fair terms and data.** [Law 93(I)/1996](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/non-ind/1996_1_93/full.html) on unfair terms, and the [Data Protection Commissioner](https://www.dataprotection.gov.cy/) for personal data.

Every EU version also follows EU consumer law: prices shown with every compulsory charge included, damage charged only at its evidenced cost and never above the excess when a damage waiver applies, no keeping the whole deposit as a penalty, and the [14-day withdrawal right not applying to car rental for set dates](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2011/83/oj). Fines and renter details follow the GDPR, and the renter signs on paper or electronically, both valid under [eIDAS](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2014/910/oj).
