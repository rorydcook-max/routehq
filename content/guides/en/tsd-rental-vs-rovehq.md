TSD Rental and RoveHQ are both car rental software, but they sit at opposite ends of the market. TSD serves dealerships and some of the largest rental companies in the world. RoveHQ is for small fleets, from one vehicle to a few dozen cars, scooters, motorbikes, quads or vans. If you're weighing them up, the main question is simply which kind of business you are.

All facts about TSD come from its own website **as of November 2026**.

## TSD Rental in brief

TSD's [homepage](https://tsdweb.com/) says it "designs and develops fleet mobility solutions for dealerships, automotive manufacturers, automotive groups, and car rental companies worldwide". Its [car rental software page](https://tsdweb.com/car-rental-software/) says it's "trusted by 10K+ car rental operators" in 75+ countries, and that the top five largest rental companies in the world use its car rental solutions.

The product covers flexible rate setups (hourly to monthly), card authorization at reservation, a fully mobile check-in process with agreements sent by text or email, license and age checks, insurance verification, utilization dashboards, and connections to channels such as Booking.com, Kayak and CarTrawler. There's an app on the App Store and Google Play. Pricing isn't published; you schedule a demo.

## Where TSD is stronger

- **Scale and track record.** Built for multi-location and nationwide fleets, and used by the biggest names in rental.
- **Dealership loaner programs.** A dedicated focus that RoveHQ doesn't have at all.
- **Channels.** Distribution through Booking.com, Kayak and CarTrawler.
- **Insurance verification** through an integration, useful in markets like the US.
- **A native app** on the App Store and Google Play.

## Where RoveHQ is stronger

- **Getting started.** Sign up yourself in a few minutes, with published prices and a free plan. No demo, sales call or onboarding fee.
- **Small fleets and two-wheelers.** Plans priced by cars and motorbikes separately, so a scooter shop pays little.
- **Payments a small shop needs.** Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay and European methods online, deposits held on the card, and cash, transfer or QR with slips checked against what's owed (including PromptPay and QRIS).
- **Agreements in the customer's language.** A rental agreement in English and the local language in 18 countries across Southeast Asia and Europe (Greek, Spanish, Italian, Thai, Indonesian and more), signed on the customer's phone.
- **Languages and chat.** 29 languages for staff and customers; LINE and Telegram in one inbox, and WhatsApp opens with the message ready.

## Who should pick which

**Choose TSD Rental if** you run a dealership loaner fleet, a multi-location rental company or a large independent, you need channel distribution and insurance verification, and you're comfortable with a demo, quote and implementation.

**Choose RoveHQ if** you have a small fleet of cars, scooters, motorbikes, quads or vans, you'd rather start today than book a demo, and you want card payments, deposits, agreements and handovers handled from your phone.
