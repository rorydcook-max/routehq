## Record a payment you were expecting

1. Go to **Money in & out** and tap **Record money** (on a phone: the + button, then **Record money in or out**).
2. RoveHQ lists the payments customers owe you first, for example "Rent for Honda Click · due today". Tap the one you got. (Tap **Show … more** if it's not in the first five.)
3. Tap **Use this payment**.
4. Check the **Amount (฿)** and **Date**, then tap **Save**.

The rent is marked as paid on the booking, and any matching job on your To do list is ticked off.

## Record money that isn't on the list

1. Tap **Record money**, then **Something else** if a list of payments shows.
2. Keep **Money in** selected.
3. Pick **What was it for?** (usually **Rental income**) and the **Vehicle**.
4. Pick the **Booking (optional)** if there is one. The customer fills in by itself.
5. Enter the amount and date, then **Save**.

While you type, RoveHQ keeps looking for a matching payment. If it asks **Is this the payment you're waiting for?**, pick it so the booking gets marked paid too.

Recorded rent before the booking existed? RoveHQ can join them up later. See [Link payments to a booking](/docs/link-payments-to-a-booking).

## From the booking instead

Open the booking, find the payment and tap **Record payment**. Enter the **Amount received**, **Date received** and **Payment method**, add a **Note** if you want, and tap **Confirm received**.

## Keep the transfer slip

- Add it under **Receipt photo (optional)** on the form, or
- Use **Upload a receipt** from the + button, choose **A payment you're waiting for**, pick the payment, tap **Continue**, then **Save**. Payments with the same amount as the slip are marked **Same amount**.

## When the customer sends the slip

Customers can pay and send their slip from their own booking page. It shows on your To do list and as **Receipt to check** on the Dashboard, and RoveHQ tells you if the amount on the slip is right. Tap **View receipt**, check the money is in your account, then **Confirm received**.

## Deposits

Pick the deposit from the waiting list, or choose **Deposit received** under **What was it for?**. Deposits aren't counted as income. They show in their own **Deposits** section. See [Deposits](/docs/deposits).

## Common questions

**Does a deposit count as income?**
No. It shows under Deposits, not in money in. It only becomes income if you keep it.
