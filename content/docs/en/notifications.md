Connections are in **Settings**, **Integrations**. What you hear about is on the **Notifications** page.

## Get alerts on your phone

1. Go to **Settings**, **Integrations**.
2. Under **Alerts on this device**, tap **Enable alerts on this device** and allow it.
3. Tap **Send a test** to check.

Do this on each phone or computer you want alerts on.

## Connect LINE

1. In **Alerts in LINE**, tap **Add friend in LINE** and add the RoveHQ account.
2. Tap **Get a connection code**, then **Send in LINE**. The code is filled in; just send it.
3. The page shows **Connected** within a few seconds. The code works for 30 minutes.

Once connected:

- **Send me alerts in LINE**: turn off to pause all LINE messages.
- **Morning summary**: around 8:00 each morning (Thailand time) you get rentals out, returns, payments due, paperwork running out and this month's income.

Tap **Save LINE settings**. **Send a test summary** sends today's summary now.

## Other places

- **Telegram**: tap **Connect Telegram**, press Start in the chat, then **check again**. Works for a group with your team too.
- **Email**: alerts and the summary by email, sent to the address in **Send to**.
- **Calendar feed**: tap **Create my calendar address**, then **Add to Google Calendar** or **Add to Apple Calendar**. Every handover and return shows up there. Keep the address private.

## Choose what you hear about

1. In **Settings**, **Integrations**, tap **Choose notifications**.
2. Tick where each alert should go: **Devices**, **LINE**, **Email** or **Telegram**. The alerts are **Booking signed**, **Payment**, **Customer request**, **New message**, **Vehicle returned**, **Your RoveHQ plan** and the **Morning summary (8:00 am)**.
3. Tap **Save**.

Dimmed boxes aren't connected yet. Tap **Connect** above them.

## Common questions

**Alerts don't work on my iPhone.**
On an iPhone, alerts only work once RoveHQ is on your home screen. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, open RoveHQ from there and turn alerts on again.
