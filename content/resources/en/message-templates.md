Most of a rental shop's day is spent typing the same messages. What's the price? Where do I pay? What time do I bring it back? This pack gives you a tested wording for each of those moments, so you or your staff can answer in seconds and sound the same every time.

## Who it's for

- Car, scooter and motorbike rental businesses that take bookings over LINE or WhatsApp
- Owners who want staff to reply consistently without writing every message themselves
- Shops renting by the day, week or month: there are messages for short trips and for monthly renters

## What's inside

Each message has the gaps marked in braces, like `{renter_name}`, `{return_time}` or `{deposit_amount}`. Replace them, check the numbers, and send. The messages are short on purpose: renters read them on a phone, often while travelling.

The money messages (deposit request, receipt, refund) always repeat the amount and the booking dates. That small habit avoids most payment confusion. The late return and damage messages start calm and factual, and point back to the agreement rather than to anyone's mood.

## How to use them as quick replies

**WhatsApp Business.** Open Settings, then Business tools, then Quick replies. Add each message with a short shortcut such as `/price` or `/deposit`. In a chat, type `/` and pick the one you need, then fill in the braces.

**LINE Official Account.** In LINE Official Account Manager, save each message as a ready reply in the chat screen so staff can insert it with a tap. For the questions you get most (price, location, opening hours), you can also set up keyword auto-responses. Check the current menu names in the LINE OA app, as they change between versions.

**No business account yet?** Paste the whole document into a note on your phone and copy from there. It still beats typing.

Pair the reminders with a clear return time in your [rental agreement](/resources/rental-agreement), and use the [handover checklist](/resources/handover-checklist) so the damage message has photos behind it.
