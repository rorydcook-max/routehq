Files are saved as you work, so you don't upload anything twice:

- vehicle photos
- the photo of a new tax receipt, Por Ror Bor or policy when you record a renewal
- garage receipts when you log a service
- the blue book (or registration document) photo from when you added the vehicle
- customer passports, licences and photos
- signed agreements, handover and return reports
- handover and return photos
- receipts you gave customers

## Find a file

1. Open **Documents**.
2. Search by name, plate or booking.
3. Or tap a filter like **Signed paperwork**, **Customer ID**, **Vehicle files**, **Receipts** or **Handover and return photos**.

Files are grouped in a card per booking, customer or vehicle, newest first. Tap a file to open it, the arrow to open the booking, customer or vehicle, **+… more** for the rest of a card, and **Show older** for more cards.

## One vehicle's files

Open the vehicle, go to **Paperwork & service**, and open **Files and documents**.

## Add a file to a vehicle

- New policy or tax receipt: **Renewed it? Enter the new date** and add the photo. RoveHQ reads the dates and cost.
- Garage receipt: **Log a service or repair** and add a **Receipt photo**.
- Vehicle photos: tap the photo at the top of the vehicle page.

## Common questions

**Where's the upload button?**
There isn't one on **Documents**. You add files where you do the work (a renewal, a service, the vehicle's photos) and they show up here.
